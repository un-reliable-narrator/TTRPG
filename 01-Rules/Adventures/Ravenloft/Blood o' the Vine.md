# Blood o' the Vine
*Adventure for Level 6 Characters* 

**Situation**. A discovery beneath the Blood of the Vine tavern in the village of Barovia has unleashed sinister magic, transforming the owners. 
**Hook**. Disguised as a human, Urwin Martikov (Medium Wereraven; see chapter 5) of the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki hires the characters to investigate why the Blood of the Vine tavern in the village of Barovia has closed, claiming he was in negotiations with the owners to buy it. Urwin offers to pay each character 500 GP. 

![tavern](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/rthw/ogMxaVTj4GqgnUqr/03-006.blood-of-the-vine-tavern.png)

### Encounters 
This adventure uses map 2.2: Blood of the Vine Tavern and consists of these encounters. 

**Corrupted Owners.** The three human sisters who owned the Blood of the Vine tavern—Alenka, Mirabel, and Sorvina—have been transformed into the following ravenous embodiments of primal hunger: Alenka ([[Relentless Slasher]]; see chapter 5) wields kitchenware and prowls the first floor. Mirabel (Medium Ghast Gravecaller) stalks the second floor with her two Ghoul servants. Sorvina ([[Unspeakable Horror]]; see chapter 5) conducts strange rituals in the basement. 

**Taproom**. Dust covers the taproom. Here, the animate corpse of Arik the barkeep mindlessly cleans pieces of shattered glassware at the bar. If attacked or otherwise interrupted, Arik falls to pieces. 

**Office**. The office in the northeast corner of the first floor is cluttered with junk and unfinished notes to the count describing the activities of the tavern’s patrons. This suggests the owners spied on the locals. If the characters have visited the tavern before, their activities are detailed. A trapdoor descends to an area beneath the tavern. 

**Private Dining Room.** The dining room in the southeast corner of the second floor is strewn with broken dishes, rotten food, and pages from a destroyed book. The pages are from the journal of Mirabel, one of the inn’s owners. Surviving entries note the following information: 
- First Entry. Mirabel’s sister Sorvina discovered a hollow hidden under the tavern. Strange ruins lie amid the dirt walls. Sorvina wants to excavate them, hoping to find a tomb filled with treasures. 
- Second Entry. Sorvina’s digging revealed strange standing stones. Mirabel worries about her sister, who’s started whispering to herself as she obsesses over learning more about the stones. 
- Third Entry. Sorvina discovered the bones of some monster at the center of the standing stones. All three sisters are having nightmares from which they wake up ravenous. Sorvina claims the hunger is a blessing. Food has lost its taste, but the inn’s patrons suddenly smell delicious. 

**Temple of Ravenkind**. A circle of ancient menhirs carved with stylized ravens and sunbursts is partially unearthed beneath the tavern. The creature that was once Sorvina is here, chanting over a monstrous skeleton of black bones with stag horns and tusks. When she notices intruders, Sorvina howls, causing the bones to rise as a [[Minotaur-Skeleton|Minotaur Skeleton]]. Both it and Sorvina fight the characters to the death. When the skeleton is defeated, its bones remain intact. 

**Cursed Remains.** The skeleton exudes evil magic. Casting Remove Curse on the monster destroys the skeleton. However, if a character touches the skeleton (while it’s animate or otherwise), its spirit whispers to the character, bidding them to end its ancient curse. Doing so destroys the skeleton and grants the character the Second Skin Dark Gift (see chapter 1). A character who already has a Dark Gift can’t accept the curse. 

**Returning to Urwin**. If the characters defeat the monsters in the tavern and return to Urwin, he pays them and shares that a benevolent relic, the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, was created at a site like the one hidden under the tavern. The circle was used later to imprison a vicious spirit of the Svalich Woods. If the characters destroyed the skeleton, Urwin rewards them with two random Quaal’s Feather Tokens. Each character also receives a bonus 1,400 XP.
