# Agent of the Ninth Quill

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Backgrounds](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/background:all) » Agent of the Ninth Quill

Source: Arcana Unleashed

**Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Intelligence, Charisma

**Feat:** [Arcane Infiltrator](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:arcane-infiltrator)

**Skill Proficiencies:** Arcana and Sleight of Hand

**Tool Proficiency:** Thieves’ Tools

**Equipment:** _Choose A or B:_ (A) Dagger, Light Hammer, Thieves’ Tools, Iron Spikes, Rope, Traveler’s Clothes, 17 GP; or (B) 50 GP

Your sharp mind and keen burgling skills led you to fall in with the Ninth Quill, a collective of thieves, saboteurs, and spies who steal magical lore and relics from others. Through your escapades - from purloining magic items and arcane tomes to sabotaging groups of mages - you’ve developed cunning skills and magical tricks to further your spy craft.
