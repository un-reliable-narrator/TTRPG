# Bejeweled Conclave Spy

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Backgrounds](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/background:all) » Bejeweled Conclave Spy

Source: Arcana Unleashed

**Ability Scores:** Dexterity, Wisdom, Charisma

**Feat:** [Arcane Eloquence](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:arcane-eloquence)

**Skill Proficiencies:** Deception and Perception

**Tool Proficiency:** Disguise Kit

**Equipment:** _Choose A or B:_ (A) Disguise Kit, Fine Clothes, Perfume, 5 GP; or (B) 50 GP

Publicly, you’re a courtier, a mediator, or an entertainer. But behind that glamorous visage is a mind honed for gathering secrets. Trained by the enchanters of the Bejeweled Conclave, you know how to use your charm and wit (plus a little magic) to infiltrate organizations, uncover information, and broker deals.
