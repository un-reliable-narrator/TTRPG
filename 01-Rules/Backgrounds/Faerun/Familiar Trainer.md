# Familiar Trainer

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Backgrounds](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/background:all) » Familiar Trainer

Source: Arcana Unleashed

**Ability Scores:** Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom

**Feat:** [Familiar Friend](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:familiar-friend)

**Skill Proficiencies:** Animal Handling and Arcana

**Tool Proficiency:** Choose one kind of Gaming Set

**Equipment:** _Choose A or B:_ (A) Quarterstaff, Gaming Set (same as above), Bedroll, Bell, String, Tinderbox, Traveler’s Clothes, Waterskin, 44 GP; or (B) 50 GP

A magical familiar bonded with you when you were young, accompanying you throughout your adolescence. Now, you can’t imagine life without your furry - or feathery, scaly, or slimy - friend at your side. You’ve learned to work in tandem with your familiar, and when adventure presents its challenges, your familiar is quick to remind you of the small joys in life.
