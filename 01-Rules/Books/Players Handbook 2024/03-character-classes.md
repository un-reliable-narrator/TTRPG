# Chapter 3: Character Classes
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024), p. 49* 


Character class provides a character's most exciting capabilities. This chapter offers twelve classes, each of which contains four subclasses—all summarized below.


## Barbarian

Storm with Rage, and wade into hand-to-hand combat. Then follow the Path of the...

- [Path of the Berserker](barbarian-path-of-the-berserker.md) to unleash raw violence.  
- [Path of the Wild Heart](barbarian-path-of-the-wild-heart.md) to manifest kinship with animals.  
- [Path of the World Tree](barbarian-path-of-the-world-tree.md) to tap into cosmic vitality.  
- [Path of the Zealot](barbarian-path-of-the-zealot.md) to rage in union with a god.  


## Bard

Perform spells that inspire and heal allies or beguile foes. Then join the College of...

- [College of Dance](bard-college-of-dance.md) to harness agility in battle.  
- [College of Glamour](bard-college-of-glamour.md) to weave beguiling Feywild magic.  
- [College of Lore](bard-college-of-lore.md) to collect knowledge and magical secrets.  
- [College of Valor](bard-college-of-valor.md) to wield weapons with spells.  


## Cleric

Invoke divine magic to heal, bolster, and smite. Then harness the...

- [Life Domain](cleric-life-domain.md) to be a master of healing.  
- [Light Domain](cleric-light-domain.md) to wield searing, warding light.  
- [Trickery Domain](cleric-trickery-domain.md) to bedevil foes with mischief.  
- [War Domain](cleric-war-domain.md) to inspire valor and chastise foes.  


## Druid

Channel nature magic to heal, shape-shift, and control the elements. Then join the Circle of the...

- [Circle of the Land](druid-circle-of-the-land.md) to draw on the magic of the environment.  
- [Circle of the Moon](druid-circle-of-the-moon.md) to adopt powerful animal forms.  
- [Circle of the Sea](druid-circle-of-the-sea.md) to channel tides and storms.  
- [Circle of the Stars](druid-circle-of-the-stars.md) to gain powers in a starry form.  


## Fighter

Master all weapons and armor. Then embody the...

- [Battle Master](fighter-battle-master.md) to use special combat maneuvers.  
- [Champion](fighter-champion.md) to strive for peak combat prowess.  
- [Eldritch Knight](fighter-eldritch-knight.md) to learn spells to aid in combat.  
- [Psi Warrior](fighter-psi-warrior.md) to augment attacks with psionic power.  


## Monk

Dart in and out of melee while striking fast and hard. Then become a Warrior of...

- [Warrior of Mercy](monk-warrior-of-mercy.md) to heal or harm with a touch.  
- [Warrior of Shadow](monk-warrior-of-shadow.md) to employ shadows for subterfuge.  
- [Warrior of the Elements](monk-warrior-of-the-elements.md) to wield elemental power.  
- [Warrior of the Open Hand](monk-warrior-of-the-open-hand.md) to master unarmed combat.  


## Paladin

Smite foes and shield allies with divine and martial might. Then swear the Oath of...

- [Oath of Devotion](paladin-oath-of-devotion.md) to emulate the angels of justice.  
- [Oath of Glory](paladin-oath-of-glory.md) to reach the heights of heroism.  
- [Oath of the Ancients](paladin-oath-of-the-ancients.md) to preserve life, joy, and nature.  
- [Oath of Vengeance](paladin-oath-of-vengeance.md) to hunt down evildoers.  


## Ranger

Weave together martial prowess, nature magic, and survival skills. Then embody the...

- [Beast Master](ranger-beast-master.md) to bond with a primal beast.  
- [Fey Wanderer](ranger-fey-wanderer.md) to manifest fey mirth and fury.  
- [Gloom Stalker](Ranger-Gloom-Stalker.md) to hunt foes that lurk in darkness.  
- [Hunter](ranger-hunter.md) to protect nature with martial versatility.  


## Rogue

Launch deadly Sneak Attacks while avoiding harm through stealth. Then embody the...

- [Arcane Trickster](rogue-arcane-trickster.md) to enhance stealth with spells.  
- [Assassin](Rogue-Assassin.md) to deliver ambushes and poison.  
- [Soulknife](rogue-soulknife.md) to strike foes with psi blades.  
- [Thief](rogue-thief.md) to master infiltration and treasure hunting.  


## Sorcerer

Wield magic innate to your being, shaping the power to your will. Then channel...

- [Aberrant Sorcery](sorcerer-aberrant Sorcery) to use strange psionic magic.  
- [Clockwork Sorcery](sorcerer-clockwork-sorcery.md) to harness cosmic forces of order.  
- [Draconic Sorcery](Sorcerer-Draconic-Sorcery.md) to breathe the magic of dragons.  
- [Wild Magic Sorcery](sorcerer-wild-magic-sorcery.md) to unleash chaos magic.  


## Warlock

Cast spells derived from occult knowledge. Then form a pact with the...

- [Archfey Patron](warlock-archfey-patron.md) to teleport and wield fey magic.  
- [Celestial Patron](Warlock-Celestial-Patron.md) to heal with heavenly magic.  
- [Fiend Patron](warlock-fiend-patron.md) to call on sinister powers.  
- [Great Old One Patron](Warlock-Great-Old-One-Patron.md) to delve into forbidden lore.  


## Wizard

Study arcane magic and master spells for every purpose. Then embody the...

- [Abjurer](Wizard-Abjurer.md) to shield allies and banish foes.  
- [Diviner](Wizard-Diviner.md) to learn the multiverse's secrets.  
- [Evoker](Wizard-Evoker.md) to create explosive effects.  
- [Illusionist](Wizard-Illusionist.md) to weave spells of deception.
