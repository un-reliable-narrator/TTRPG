### Action Surge (Level 2)

You can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a moment. On your turn, you can take one additional action, except the [Magic](app://obsidian.md/Magic.md) action.

Once you use this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a [Short](app://obsidian.md/short-rest.md) or [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md). Starting at level 17, you can use it twice before a rest but only once on a turn.
