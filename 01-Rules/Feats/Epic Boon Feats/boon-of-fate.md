# Boon of Fate
*Epic Boon Feat*

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 210. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*  

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Improve Fate.** When you or another creature within 60 feet of you succeeds on or fails a [D20 Test](d20-test.md), you can roll `dice: 2d4` and apply the total rolled as a bonus or penalty to the `dice: d20` roll. Once you use this benefit, you can't use it again until you roll [Initiative](01-Rules/Rules/Initiative.md) or finish a [Short](short-rest.md) or [Long Rest](long-rest.md).
