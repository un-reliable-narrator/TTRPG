# Interception
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 209*  

**Prerequisite**: Fighting Style Feature

When a creature you can see hits another creature within 5 feet of you with an attack roll, you can take a [Reaction](Reaction.md) to reduce the damage dealt to the target by `dice: 1d10` plus your [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md). You must be holding a [Shield](01-Rules/Spells/Core/Level%201/Shield.md) or a Simple or Martial weapon to use this [Reaction](Reaction.md).
