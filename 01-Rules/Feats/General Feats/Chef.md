# Chef
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 202*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution or Wisdom by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Cook's Utensils.** You gain proficiency with [Cook's Utensils](cooks-Utensils.md) if you don't already have it.

**Replenishing Meal.** As part of a [Short Rest](short-rest.md), you can cook special food if you have ingredients and [Cook's Utensils](cooks-Utensils.md) on hand. You can prepare enough of this food for a number of creatures equal to 4 plus your [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md). At the end of the [Short Rest](short-rest.md), any creature who eats the food and spends one or more Hit Dice to regain [Hit Points](hit-points.md) regains an extra `dice: 1d8` [Hit Points](hit-points.md).

**Bolstering Treats.** With 1 hour of work or when you finish a [Long Rest](long-rest.md), you can cook a number of treats equal to your [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md) if you have ingredients and [Cook's Utensils](cooks-Utensils.md) on hand. These special treats last 8 hours after being made. A creature can use a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md) to eat one of those treats to gain a number of [Temporary-Hit-Points](Temporary-Hit-Points.md) equal to your [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md).
