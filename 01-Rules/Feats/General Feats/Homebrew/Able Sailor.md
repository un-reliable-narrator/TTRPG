# Able Sailor
*Feat*

You are completely at home aboard a ship, gaining the following benefits:

- Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
- You gain proficiency in [Acrobatics](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills).
- When you make an [Acrobatics](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills) check to keep your footing on a ship, a [Sleight of Hand](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills) of hand skill check dealing with knots or ropes, or any check using Vehicles (Water), you double your proficiency bonus to the check.
- Climbing no longer costs you extra movement.

Source: *Save_vs_DM*
