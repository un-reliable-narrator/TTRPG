# Lock-On
*Feat*[^1]

*You focus your aim and killing intent onto one creature to target their weak points.* 

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity by 1, up to a maximum of 20.

**Mark Target.** After you hit a creature with a ranged weapon attack roll, you can marks it. You have advantage on attack rolls against marked creatures. All your marks end immediately if you miss an attack roll.

Source: *Dtzik (me)*

[^1]: Could be a class trait?
