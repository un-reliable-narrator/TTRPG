# Sharpshooter
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 207*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th; Dexterity 13 or higher

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Bypass Cover.** Your ranged attacks with weapons ignore [[Half Cover]] and [Three-Quarters Cover](01-Rules/Tables/RAW/Combat/Cover.md).

**Firing in Melee.** Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn't impose [[01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on your attack rolls with Ranged weapons.

**Long Shots.** Attacking at long range doesn't impose [[01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on your attack rolls with Ranged weapons.
