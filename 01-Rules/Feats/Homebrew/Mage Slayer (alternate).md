# Mage Slayer (alternate)
Source: Homebrew (by Dtzik)

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength or Dexterity by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Concentration Breaker.** When you damage a creature that is [Concentrating](Conditions.md#Concentration), it has [[01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on the saving throw it makes to maintain [Concentration](Conditions.md#Concentration).

**Disarm Caster.** When a creature within your reach casts a spell, you can make an opportunity attack against that creature before the spell resolves. On a hit, if the target was using somatic or material components (including a spell focus) in the spell's casting, it does not resolve, and the action, bonus action or reaction to cast it is wasted. 
*(Optional: On a critical hit, you sever the limb performing somatic components or holding material components.)*
