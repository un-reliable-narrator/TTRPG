# Exceptional Memory
Source: Homebrew (by Dtzik)

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

You can remember almost all of your life in vivid detail. 

You gain the following benefits and flaw.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Intelligence by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Absorb Information.** You don't forget anything you've seen, heard or read, like names, codes or the exact words of a letter. Additionally, you can halve the time it takes you to read and understand any text. 

**Well of Knowledge.** You have advantage on Intelligence checks.

**Backward Gaze (Flaw)**. *"I obsess over my past mistakes, which often clouds my view of the present moment.*
