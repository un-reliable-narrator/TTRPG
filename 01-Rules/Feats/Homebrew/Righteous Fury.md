# Righteous Fury
Source: Homebrew by *Dtzik*

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, not a Barbarian)*

You have an intense, if not necessarily reasonable, sense of justice. Seeing it violated can cause you to fly into powerful and terrifying wrath.

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Strength, Constitution or Charisma by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Rage.** Once, as a bonus action, you can enter into a rage chosen from the options below. It lasts for 1 minute or until you become incapacitated. 

- **Reckless Abandon.** You have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saves. Additionally, you have advantage on attack rolls, but attack rolls against you also have advantage.
- **Righteous Determination.** You have advantage on Charisma saves, as well as saves made to avoid or end the charmed or frighted condition. Additionally, you have advantage on Charisma ([[Intimidation]]) checks.
- **Unstoppable Fury.** If you would be reduced to 0 hit points, you can make a Constitution save (DC: 5 + damage taken). On a success, you retain 1 hp instead. You can only benefit from this ability once per Rage.

Once you enter a rage, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest or incur a level of exhaustion to refresh it.
