# Arcane Infiltrator

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Feats](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/feat:all) » Arcane Infiltrator

Source: Arcana Unleashed

_Origin Feat_

You gain the following benefits.

**Cantrip.** You learn the [Friends](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:friends) cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).

**Cunning Diversion.** You can take the Dodge action as a Bonus Action. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.
