# Dancer

**Prerequisite:** Dexterity score of 13 or higher

_Origin Feat (Prerequisite: Dexterity score of 13 or higher)_

You gain the following benefits.

_**Dance Magic.**_ You learn the [Dancing Lights](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2057-dancing-lights) cantrip. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this feat’s spells.

_**Flexibility.**_ You can add your Dexterity modifier to Charisma (Performance) checks.

_**Shuffle Away.**_ You can move up to 10 feet as a reaction when a creature moves within 5 feet of you. This movement doesn’t provoke opportunity attacks.

Source: *LightGauge10 @DnDBeyond*
