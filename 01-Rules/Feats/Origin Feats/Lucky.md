# Lucky
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 201*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Luck Points.** You have a number of Luck Points equal to your [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md) and can spend the points on the benefits below. You regain your expended Luck Points when you finish a [Long Rest](long-rest.md).

**Advantage.** When you roll a `dice: d20` for a [D20 Test](d20-test.md), you can spend 1 Luck Point to give yourself [[Advantage|Advantage]] on the roll.

**Disadvantage.** When a creature rolls a `dice: d20` for an attack roll against you, you can spend 1 Luck Point to impose [[01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on that roll.
