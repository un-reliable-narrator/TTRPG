# Musician
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 201*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Instrument Training.** You gain proficiency with three Musical Instruments of your choice.

**Encouraging Song.** As you finish a [Short](short-rest.md) or [Long Rest](long-rest.md), you can play a song on a [Musical Instrument](Musical-Instrument.md) with which you have proficiency and give [Heroic Inspiration](Heroic-Inspiration.md) to allies who hear the song. The number of allies you can affect in this way equals your [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md).
