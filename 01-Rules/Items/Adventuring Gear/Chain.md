# Chain
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 5 gp
- **Weight**: 10.0 lbs.

As a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action, you can wrap a Chain around an unwilling creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [Grappled](Conditions.md#Grappled), [Incapacitated](Conditions.md#Incapacitated), or [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Strength ([Athletics](Athletics.md)) check. If the creature's legs are bound, the creature has the [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) condition until it escapes. Escaping the Chain requires the creature to make a successful DC 18 Dexterity ([Acrobatics](Acrobatics.md)) check as an action. Bursting the Chain requires a successful DC 20 Strength ([Athletics](Athletics.md)) check as an action.

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 224. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
