# Hooded Lantern
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 5 gp
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

A Hooded Lantern burns Oil as fuel to cast [Bright Light](bright-light.md) in a 30-foot radius and [Dim Light](dim-light.md) for an additional 30 feet. As a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md), you can lower the hood, reducing the light to [Dim Light](dim-light.md) in a 5-foot radius, or raise it again.

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 226. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
