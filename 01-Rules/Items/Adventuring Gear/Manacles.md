# Manacles
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 2 gp
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

As a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action, you can use Manacles to bind an unwilling Small or Medium creature within 5 feet of yourself that has the [Grappled](Conditions.md#Grappled), [Incapacitated](Conditions.md#Incapacitated), or [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) condition if you succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity ([Sleight of Hand](Sleight%20of%20Hand.md)) check. While bound, a creature has [[01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on attack rolls, and the creature is [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) if the Manacles are attached to a chain or hook that is fixed in place. Escaping the Manacles requires a successful DC 20 Dexterity ([Sleight of Hand](Sleight%20of%20Hand.md)) check as an action. Bursting them requires a successful DC 25 Strength ([Athletics](Athletics.md)) check as an action.

Each set of Manacles comes with a key. Without the key, a creature can use [Thieves' Tools](Thieves-Tools.md) to pick the Manacles' lock with a successful DC 15 Dexterity ([Sleight of Hand](Sleight%20of%20Hand.md)) check.

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 226. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
