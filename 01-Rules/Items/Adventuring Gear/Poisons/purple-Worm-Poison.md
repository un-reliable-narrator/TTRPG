# Purple Worm Poison
*Adventuring gear, poison (injury)*  


- **Properties**: [Poison](Item-Properties.md#Poison)
- **Cost**: 2,000 gp

A creature subjected to Purple Worm Poison makes a DC 21 Constitution saving throw, taking 35 (`dice: 10d6`) Poison damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 91. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span>*
