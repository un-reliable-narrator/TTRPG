# Earring of Message

Source: Critical Role - Call of the Netherdeep

_Wondrous item, common_

The blue crystal of this earring is wrapped with delicate copper wire. The earring has 5 charges. While wearing it, you can use an action to expend 1 charge and cast the [Message](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/spell:message) spell. The earring regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.
