# Pressure Capsule

Source: Ghosts of Saltmarsh #5e2014 

_Wondrous item, common_

This small capsule is made of beeswax blended with sand and a variety of enchanted water plants. A creature who consumes a _pressure capsule_ ignores the effects of swimming at depths greater than 100 feet (see "Unusual Environments" in chapter 5 of the _Dungeon Master's Guide_).
