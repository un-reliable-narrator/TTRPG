# Lock of Trickery
*Wondrous item, common*  



This lock appears to be an ordinary [Lock](Lock.md) (of the type described in chapter 6 of the "Player's Handbook") and comes with a single key. The tumblers in this lock magically adjust to thwart burglars. Dexterity checks made to pick the lock have [[01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]].

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 275*
