# Moon-Touched Sword
*Common*  
![](items/XDMG/Moon-Touched Sword.webp#right)


In [Darkness](01-Rules/Rules/Darkness.md), the unsheathed blade of this weapon sheds moonlight, creating [Bright Light](bright-light.md) in a 15-foot radius and [Dim Light](dim-light.md) for an additional 15 feet.
