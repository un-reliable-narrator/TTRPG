# Orb of Time
*Wondrous item, common*  


- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

This orb can be used as an Arcane Focus.

While holding the orb, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to determine whether it is morning, afternoon, evening, or nighttime. This property functions only on the Material Plane.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 284*
