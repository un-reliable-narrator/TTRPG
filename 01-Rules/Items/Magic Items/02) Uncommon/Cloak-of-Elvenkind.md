# Cloak of Elvenkind
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  


While you wear this cloak, Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) checks made to perceive you have [[01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]], and you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Dexterity ([Stealth](Stealth.md)) checks.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
