# Ring of Candor

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Magic Items](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:all) » Ring of Candor

Source: Arcana Unleashed - Deadfall

_Ring, Uncommon_

While wearing this simple metal band, you gain a +5 bonus to Wisdom (Insight) checks. In addition, you can cast [Zone of Truth](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:zone-of-truth) (save DC 13) from the ring. You can’t cast [Zone of Truth](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:zone-of-truth) in this way again until the next dawn.
