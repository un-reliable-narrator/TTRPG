# Wand of Enthralling

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Magic Items](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:all) » Wand of Enthralling

Source: Arcana Unleashed - Deadfall

_Wand, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Spellcaster)_

This wand has 4 charges and regains 1d4 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding it, you can expend 1 charge to cast [Enthrall](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:enthrall) (save DC 15) from it.
