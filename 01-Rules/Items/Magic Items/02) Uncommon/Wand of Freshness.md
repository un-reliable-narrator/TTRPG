# Wand of Freshness

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Magic Items](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:all) » Wand of Freshness

Source: Arcana Unleashed

_Wand, Uncommon_

This wand has 4 charges and regains 1d4+1 expended charges daily at dawn. While holding it, you can expend 1 charge to cast the [Purify Food and Drink](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:purify-food-and-drink) spell from it.
