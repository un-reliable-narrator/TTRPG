# Dust of Dryness
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  



This small packet contains `1d6 + 4` pinches of dust. As a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action, you can sprinkle a pinch of the dust over water, turning up to a 15-foot [Cube](cube-area-of-effect.md) of water into one marble-sized pellet, which floats or rests near where the dust was sprinkled. The pellet's weight is negligible. A creature can take a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action to smash the pellet against a hard surface, causing the pellet to shatter and release the water the dust absorbed. Doing so destroys the pellet and ends its magic.

As a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action, you can sprinkle a pinch of the dust on an Elemental within 5 feet of yourself that is composed mostly of water (such as a [Water Elemental](water-Elemental.md) or a [Water Weird](water-Weird.md)). Such a creature exposed to a pinch of the dust makes a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking `dice: 10d6` Necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 255. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
