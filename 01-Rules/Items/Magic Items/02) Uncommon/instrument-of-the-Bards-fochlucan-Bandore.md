# Instrument of the Bards, Fochlucan Bandore
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a bard)*  


An Instrument of the Bards is superior to an ordinary instrument in every way. Seven types of these instruments exist, each named after a bard college. A creature that attempts to play the instrument without being attuned to it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or take `dice: 2d4` Psychic damage.

You can play the Fochlucan Bandore to cast one of the following spells: [Fly](Fly.md), [Invisibility](Invisibility.md), [Levitate](Levitate.md), [Protection from Evil and Good](Protection-from-Evil-and-Good.md), [Entangle](Entangle.md), [Faerie Fire](Faerie-Fire.md), [Shillelagh](Shillelagh.md), and [Speak with Animals](Speak-with-Animals.md). Once the Fochlucan Bandore has been used to cast a spell, it can't be used to cast that spell again until the next dawn. The spells use your spellcasting ability and spell save DC.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 272*
