# Potion of Psychic Resistance
*Potion, uncommon*  


- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, you have [Resistance](01-Rules/Rules/Resistance.md) to psychic damage for 1 hour.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 289. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
