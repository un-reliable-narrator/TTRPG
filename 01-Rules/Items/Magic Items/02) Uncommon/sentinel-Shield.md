# Sentinel Shield
*Armor ([Shield](01-Rules/Spells/Core/Level%201/Shield.md)), uncommon*  


- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

While holding this Shield, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on [Initiative](01-Rules/Rules/Initiative.md) rolls and Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) checks. The Shield is emblazoned with a symbol of an eye.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 303. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
