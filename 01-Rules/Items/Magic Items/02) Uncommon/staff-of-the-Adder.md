# Staff of the Adder
*Staff, weapon, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Staff of the Adder.webp#right)

- **Damage**:
  - One-handed: 1d6 bludgeoning
  - Two-handed: 1d8 bludgeoning
- **Properties**: [Versatile](Item-Properties.md#Versatile)
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

As a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md), you can turn the head of this staff into that of an animate, venomous snake for 1 minute or revert the staff to its inanimate form.

When you take the [[Attack]] action, you can make one of the attack rolls using the animated snake head, which has a reach of 5 feet. Apply your [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md) and Wisdom modifier to the attack roll. On a hit, the target takes `dice: 1d6` Piercing damage and `dice: 3d6` Poison damage.

The snake head can be attacked while it is animate. It has AC 15, HP 20, and [Immunity](01-Rules/Rules/Immunity.md) to Poison and Psychic damage. If the head drops to 0 [Hit Points](hit-points.md), the staff is destroyed. As long as it's not destroyed, the staff regains all lost [Hit Points](hit-points.md) when it reverts to its inanimate form.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 310*
