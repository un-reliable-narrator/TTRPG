# Arcane Chantelaine

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [Magic Items](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:all) » Arcane Chantelaine

Source: Arcana Unleashed

_Wondrous Item, Rare_

This ornate clasp has six spectral keys attached to it. As a Magic action, you can touch one of the keys to a closed door, window, gate, container, or hatch to cast the [Arcane Lock](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:arcane-lock) spell on it. Once a key is used, it disappears. When all six keys have disappeared, the chatelaine disappears.
