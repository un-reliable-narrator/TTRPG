# Bonecounter

Source: Sleeping Dragon's Wake

_Weapon (mace), rare_

This weapon is a magical +2 mace called _Bonecounter_. Whenever this weapon is used to destroy an undead creature, a single silver piece appears in the wielder’s pocket.
