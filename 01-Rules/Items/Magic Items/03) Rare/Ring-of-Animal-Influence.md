# Ring of Animal Influence
*Ring, rare*  
![](items/XDMG/Ring of Animal Influence.webp#right)


This ring has 3 charges, and it regains `dice: 1d3` expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can expend 1 charge to cast one of the following spells (save DC 13) from it:

- [Animal Friendship](Animal-Friendship.md)  
- [Fear](Fear.md) (affects Beasts only)  
- [Speak with Animals](Speak-with-Animals.md)  

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 292. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
