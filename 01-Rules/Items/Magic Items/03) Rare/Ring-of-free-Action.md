# Ring of Free Action
*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Ring of Free Action.webp#right)


While you wear this ring, [Difficult Terrain](difficult-terrain.md) doesn't cost you extra movement. In addition, magic can neither reduce any of your Speeds nor cause you to have the [Paralyzed](Conditions.md#Paralyzed) or [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) condition.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
