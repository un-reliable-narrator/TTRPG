# Scroll of Protection (Celestials)
*Scroll, rare*  



Using a [Magic](Magic.md) action to read the scroll creates a 5-foot [Emanation](emanation-area-of-effect.md) originating from you. For 5 minutes, {{item.detail2}} can't enter or affect anything in the area. However, if you move in such a way that an Celestial would be inside the area, the effect ends.

As a [Magic](Magic.md) action, a creature within 5 feet of the [Emanation](emanation-area-of-effect.md) can attempt to overcome it, which forces the creature to make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a successful save, the creature ceases to be affected by the [Emanation](emanation-area-of-effect.md).

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 302*
