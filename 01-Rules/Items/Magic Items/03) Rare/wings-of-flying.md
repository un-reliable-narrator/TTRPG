# Wings of Flying
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Wings of Flying.webp#right)


While wearing this cloak, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to turn the cloak into a pair of wings on your back. The wings lasts for 1 hour or until you end the effect early as a [Magic](Magic.md) action. The wings give you a [Fly Speed](fly-speed.md) of 60 feet. If you are aloft when the wings disappear, you fall. When the wings disappear, you can't use them again for `dice: 1d12` hours.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 324. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
