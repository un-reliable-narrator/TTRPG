# Cloak of Arachnida
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*  



This fine garment is made of black silk interwoven with faint, silvery threads. While wearing it, you gain the following benefits.

## Poison Resistance

You have [Resistance](01-Rules/Rules/Resistance.md) to Poison damage.

## Spider Climb

You have a [Climb Speed](climb-speed.md) equal to your [Speed](01-Rules/Rules/Speed.md) and can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings, while leaving your hands free.

## Spider Walk

You can't be caught in webs of any sort and can move through webs as if they were [Difficult Terrain](difficult-terrain.md).

## Web

You can cast [Web](Web.md) (save DC 13). The web created by the spell fills twice its normal area. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
