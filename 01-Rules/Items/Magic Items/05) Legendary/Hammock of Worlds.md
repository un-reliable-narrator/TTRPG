# Hammock of Worlds

Source: Journeys through the Radiant Citadel

_Wondrous item, legendary_

The _Hammock of Worlds_ is a colorful hammock woven with traditional Ataguan designs. It can be used only by a member of the order known as the Green Doctors.

You can use an action to unfold and place the _Hammock of Worlds_ on a solid surface, whereupon it creates a two-way portal to the Ghost Orchid Tepui in the Feywild or to the Drought Elder in the Far Realm (the user's choice). You can use an action to close an open portal by taking hold of the edges of the cloth and folding it up. Once the _Hammock of Worlds_ has opened a portal, it can't do so again for 1d8 hours.
