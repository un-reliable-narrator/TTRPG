# Ring of Invisibility
*Ring, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Ring of Invisibility.webp#right)


While wearing this ring, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to give yourself the [Invisible](Conditions.md#Invisible) condition. You remain [Invisible](Conditions.md#Invisible) until the ring is removed or until you take a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md) to become visible again.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
