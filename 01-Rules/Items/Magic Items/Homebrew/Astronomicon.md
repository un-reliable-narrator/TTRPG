# The Astronomicon
*Wondrous Item, Artifact*

- **Weight:** 1.0 kg.

***Book of Power.*** After the reader absorbs the book's benefits, the pages go blank and it loses all magical properties. The reader can scribe a single copy of the book in 1.096 days (assuming 8 hours of work a day - the exact time needed is 8766 hours, 9 minutes, and 10 seconds). Should the reader be killed, a new copy manifests in a random location on this plane within 1d100 days.

This book contains insights on the cosmos and it's celestial bodies. Reading it, a user who meets the prerequisites may gain one of the following benefits.

*Note: These traits may be similar or identical to certain (sub)class features. At GM discretion, a creature who would have both the book's benefit as well as it's corresponding class feature may replace one with an Ability Score Improvement.*

#### ​​Cosmic Omen
*Prerequisite: 16+ Wisdom*
​Whenever you finish a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md), you can consult your Star Map for omens and roll a die. Until you finish your next [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md), you gain access to a special [Reaction](app://obsidian.md/reaction.md) based on whether you rolled an even or an odd number on the die:

- **Weal (even).** Whenever a creature you can see within 30 feet of you is about to make a [D20 Test](app://obsidian.md/d20-test.md), you can take a [Reaction](app://obsidian.md/reaction.md) to roll 1d6 and add the number rolled to the total.
- **Woe (odd).** Whenever a creature you can see within 30 feet of you is about to make a [D20 Test](app://obsidian.md/d20-test.md), you can take a [Reaction](app://obsidian.md/reaction.md) to roll 1d6 and subtract the number rolled from the total.

You can use this [Reaction](app://obsidian.md/reaction.md) a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md).

#### Lunar Empowerment
*Prerequisite: Arcana proficiency or spellcasting/pact magic feature.*
The power of a lunar phase saturates your being. Whenever you finish a Long Rest, roll 1d3. Until you finish your next long rest, you gain access to the powers of the corresponding lunar phase.

- **1. Full Moon:** You shed bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. In addition, you and creatures of your choice have advantage on saving throws while within the bright light you shed.
- **2. New Moon:** You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. In addition, while you are in dim light or darkness, attack rolls have disadvantage against you.
- **3. Crescent Moon:** You have resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.
