# Ring of the Goat
*Ring, common (requires attunement by a Attuned after a short rest with the item)*

While wearing this carved horn ring, you can consume virtually any organic matter, such as grass, leaves, twigs, linen, leather, etc., and find it as tasty and sustaining as normal food.

Notes: Attuned after a short rest with the item
