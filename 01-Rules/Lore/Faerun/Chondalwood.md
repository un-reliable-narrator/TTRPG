## History[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenrealms.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChondalwood%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")

**Chondalwood** was a large forest located in [Chondath](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondath "Chondath") in the [Vilhon Reach](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vilhon_Reach "Vilhon Reach"), stretching from the [Nunwood](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nunwood "Nunwood") to the [Nagaflow](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nagaflow "Nagaflow") River.[2](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-FRCS3e-p217-2) It was also known as the **Satyrwood**. The village of [Elbulder](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Elbulder "Elbulder") was to the south of the forest.[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-VR-p64-1)

The forest was once home to the [green elf](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Green_elf "Green elf") realm of [Nikerymath](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nikerymath "Nikerymath").[³](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-LEoF-p119-3) It contains the hidden city of [Rucien-Xan](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rucien-Xan "Rucien-Xan").[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-VR-p64-1)

## Inhabitants[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenrealms.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FChondalwood%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")

[![A  of the Satyrwood keeping an eye on .](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/3/31/Satyrwood.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20211228215325)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/3/31/Satyrwood.png/revision/latest?cb=20211228215325)
_A [satyr](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Satyr "Satyr") of the Satyrwood keeping an eye on [humans](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Human "Human")._

The Chondalwood was the home of [wild elves](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wild_elves "Wild elves").[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-FRCS3e-p217-2)

As of [1368 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1368_DR "1368 DR"), troops of [satyrs](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Satyr "Satyr") patrolled the [Old Road](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Road "Old Road"), scaring travelers into making haste out of the forest or to go around it, and the forest was also inhabited by [voadkyn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Voadkyn "Voadkyn") (wood giants) and [centaurs](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Centaur "Centaur").[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-VR-p64-1) As of [1372 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1372_DR "1372 DR"), savage druids lived in the forest and posed a threat to anyone approaching them. Clans of [ghostwise halfling](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ghostwise_halfling "Ghostwise halfling") roamed about the forest, never staying in one spot for too long.[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-FRCS3e-p217-2) Various plant creatures such as [shambling mounds](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shambling_mounds "Shambling mounds") and [tendriculos](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tendriculos "Tendriculos") also lived here.[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-FRCS3e-p217-2) All of these intelligent races had one goal in mind: to prevent [human](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Human "Human") encroachment.[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-VR-p64-1)

The [Eldreth Veluuthra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Eldreth_Veluuthra "Eldreth Veluuthra") were active in this forest, and as of [1374 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1374_DR "1374 DR"), generally received support from the forest's inhabitants such as the centaurs, ghostwise halflings and satyrs. The wild elves in the forest provided the organization's agents with shelter.[4](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-CoR-p79-4) [Talindra Amalith](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Talindra_Amalith "Talindra Amalith") of the [Vel'Nikeryma](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vel%27Nikeryma "Vel'Nikeryma") (the Eldreth Veluuthra's guiding council) sought out new wild elf recruits in this area.[5](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood#cite_note-CoR-p80-5)
