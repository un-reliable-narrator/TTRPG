# Fort Arran
*Sespechan Outpost, Faerun*

**Fort Arran**, sometimes referred to by its old name **Arranford**, was a small town, turned outpost that stood on the [Golden Road](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Road "Golden Road") between [Sespech](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sespech "Sespech") and the the capital of [Chondath](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondath "Chondath") – [Arrabar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arrabar "Arrabar").[4](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p55-4)[6](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-map-6)

> “_This fort is the Baron's first line of defense against an attack launched from Arrabar. It's served him well over the years, forcing Chondath send troops across the river at inconvenient locations where accidents are common-place_”
> — [Adamar Session](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Adamar_Session "Adamar Session"), [bard](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bard "Bard") of [Starmantle](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Starmantle "Starmantle")[7](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p28-7)

## Geography[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenrealms.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFort_Arran%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")

Arranford stood at the mouth of [River Arran](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/River_Arran "River Arran"), near where the northwest edge of [Chondalwood](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chondalwood "Chondalwood") reached towards the river.[8](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-EmpSe-p142-8) The fort stood by the bridge across the river that was part of the Golden Road. The city of Arrabar laid further north and [Ormpetarr](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr "Ormpetarr") – south.[6](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-map-6) Smoke from chimneys of Arranford could be seen from a great distance.[9](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-VK-ch10-9)

## Government[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenrealms.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFort_Arran%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")

_Circa_ the [Year of Wild Magic, 1372 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1372_DR "1372 DR"), Fort Arran was placed in the hands of General [Marcius Stonehall](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Marcius_Stonehall "Marcius Stonehall"), an old experienced godly [dwarf](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dwarf "Dwarf"). Marcius was a member of Baron Foesmasher's adventuring troupe, along with the Lord-Mayor [Gavilon Jostins](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gavilon_Jostins "Gavilon Jostins") of [Elbulder](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Elbulder "Elbulder") during the days of their youth.[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-FR3e-p219-1)

## Trade[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenrealms.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFort_Arran%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D3&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")

Fort Arran had little trade, but it attracted attention during the annual celebration of Chondath using power in the wake of the [Rotting War](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rotting_War "Rotting War"). The Rotting Dance festival was gleefully celebrated in the outpost, often joined by Baron Foesmasher himself.[10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p56-10)

## Defenses[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenrealms.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFort_Arran%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")

The fort's garrison counted a thousand loyal Sespechan soldiers who were ready to fall in defense of their free nation. Every soldier knew that Fort Arran's duty was not to win a potential conflict but rather to delay an invading force.[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-FR3e-p219-1) The garrison included healers.[11](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-VK-ch13-11) The General regularly hired trusted adventuring companies to travel to Arrabar and keep an eye on any troop or war supplies being deployed.[7](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p28-7)

## History[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fforgottenrealms.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFort_Arran%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")

[![A map depicting the pre-Fort Arran town of Arranford in the mid-14th century .](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/d/dc/Old_Fort_Arran.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/290?cb=20221001030405)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/forgottenrealms/images/d/dc/Old_Fort_Arran.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20221001030405)

_A map depicting the pre-Fort Arran town of Arranford in the mid-14th century [DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/DR "DR")._

Long before Arranford stood at the mouth of River Arran, the lands that stretched between the river and [Nagawater](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nagawater "Nagawater") were a nation of [Maurmurra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Maurmurra "Maurmurra"). It was dissolved with the death of its leader in the [Year of the Curse, 882 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/882_DR "882 DR").[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tGHOR-p108-2)[3](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-mag-p54-3)

Prior to the [Year of the Wave, 1364 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1364_DR "1364 DR"), Fort Arran was a small town along the Golden Road called Arranford. Briefly, the town became a battle site following Baron [Thuragar Foesmasher](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Thuragar_Foesmasher "Thuragar Foesmasher")'s rise to power. The new ruler ordered his predecessor [Ricjolo Tomrase](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ricjolo_Tomrase "Ricjolo Tomrase") to return to his puppeteers in Arrabar. In retaliation, Chondath sent a "peace-keeping" army to Ormpetarr.[4](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p55-4) The new Baron, of course, was ready for the show of force and stationed a force in the town of Arranford that stood between two cities. As the short battle ended with Foesmasher's triumph. It resulted in Sespech's true independence and prosperity in the following years.[12](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p27-12) To place a buffer in case of future aggression, Arranford became Fort Arran. The outpost that held an army of almost 1,000 souls to the chagrin of the Lord of Arrabar.[4](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p55-4)

As of the [Year of the Tankard, 1370 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1370_DR "1370 DR"), Fort Arran remained a warning station in case of wars erupting in the tumultuous region of the Vilhon Reach. As such, messengers were always at the ready to be dispatched to [Mimph](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mimph "Mimph"), Elbulder, and Ormpetarr with a warning for the rest of the free nation.[5](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-tVR-p57-5) Those stationed at the outpost considered the service to be a great honor.[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran#cite_note-FR3e-p219-1)
