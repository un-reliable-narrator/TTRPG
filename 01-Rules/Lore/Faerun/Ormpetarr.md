**Ormpetarr** was the capital city of [Sespech](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sespech "Sespech") in the [Vilhon Reach](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vilhon_Reach "Vilhon Reach").[13](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p218-13) During the century that followed the _[Spellplague](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Spellplague "Spellplague")_, Ormpetarr was known as the **City of the Scar**.[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRPG4e-p126-2)

## Description

Ormpetarr was an ancient metropolis, protected from the outside dangers by a thick wall.[10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p220-10) The walls stretched all around the city, including the side of Ormpetarr that fronted the [Nagawater](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Nagawater "Nagawater"). Impressive stonework was interrupted every now and then by thick wooden gates and piers that stretch from each. Docking vessels were guided to free docks by small tugboats. Each harbor gate was manned by a duo of soldiers who served as city entry customs.[14](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-VipKis-ch4-14) Past the harbor stood the Warehouse Row district.[15](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p29-15) The city was filled with ancient brass spires, famed for their ability to reflect the setting sun like flame.[16](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-PoS-p194-16) Its straight streets were tightly packed with buildings, homes, and businesses and lit with ordinary [lanterns](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lantern "Lantern") through the night. [Helm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Helm "Helm") was the primary deity of the city which was obvious by his symbol on the guard's uniforms and wide city squares decorated with [silver](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Silver "Silver") [gauntlet](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gauntlet "Gauntlet")-bearing statues.[14](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-VipKis-ch4-14)

As of [1396 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1396_DR "1396 DR") many of the brass spires lay broken and strewn down the rocky side of a cliff.[16](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-PoS-p194-16) The city degraded and became notoriously lawless settlement.[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRPG4e-p126-2)

The metropolis was built on the eastern shores of the Nagawater lake in the [Golden Plains](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Plains "Golden Plains"), along the [Golden Road](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Road "Golden Road") trade way.[10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p220-10) Ormpetarr's closest neighbors along the Golden Road were [Fort Arran](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran "Fort Arran") and the city of [Arrabar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arrabar "Arrabar") to the north and [Innarlith](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Innarlith "Innarlith") to the south.[17](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-map-17)

## Government

In the 14th century DR, Ormpetarr was ruled by Baron [Thuragar Foesmasher](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Thuragar_Foesmasher "Thuragar Foesmasher"), supported by the city's merchants, who stood in dire opposition to the nation of Chondath.[10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p220-10) Before his rule, Ormpetarr was a barony of Chondath that seceded following the [Rotting War](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rotting_War "Rotting War"). After gaining independence, Ormpetarr was ruled by a speckling of self-proclaimed barons, lucky adventurers, and Chondath's puppets.[9](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p57-9) Baron Thuragar Foesmasher ruled with open-door policies and had daily scheduled public court sessions, in contrast to ruling from behind a closed door done by his predecessors.[18](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p58-18)

In [1479 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1479_DR "1479 DR"), there was no official government. but power was shared uneasily by the [Order of Blue Flame](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Blue_Flame "Order of Blue Flame"), forces loyal to [Tyrangal](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrangal "Tyrangal") and the huge Monastery of Kelemvor.[19](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TEoC-19)

### Laws

Between the _Spellplague_ and the Second Sundering, Ormpetarr was guided by a single unspoken law: only types of a crime punishable by the unofficial rulers were crimes that interfered with pilgrims' flow.[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRPG4e-p126-2)

## Trade

Ormpetarr was a banking center and handled all trade on the Golden Road and a busy caravan stop.[20](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p90-20) It was surrounded by lush fertile fields growing grains for export. Other major exports included [fish](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fish "Fish"), and [horses](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Horse "Horse"). Merchant vessels that sailed the Nagawater often carried import [wine](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Wine "Wine").[10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p220-10)[9](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p57-9) Fishermen of Ormpetarr were assisted by [water nagas](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Water_naga "Water naga") of the Nagawater lake. The monstrosities drove fish into Ormpetarr's nets, while the fishers respected nagas' territory indicated by buoys lit with _[continual light](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Continual_light "Continual light")_ spell.[21](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p59-21)

Between the _Spellplague_ and the Second Sundering, Ormpetarr existed thanks to pilgrims and travelers that passed through the region. Its streets were dotted with establishments that catered to pilgrims: taverns, inns, and pawn shops were main businesses within the shrunken city bounds.[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRPG4e-p126-2) Ormpetarr also attracted mages and mystics who sought to explore ruins left behind by the magical cataclysm.[11](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCG-p193-11) During that time, Ormpetarr was too chaotic and too little to offer to maintain active trade relationships with other cities. One trade partner of note, however, was [Riatavin](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Riatavin "Riatavin").[12](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCG-p179-12)

## Defenses

Prior to [1385 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1385_DR "1385 DR"), Ormpetarr was home to the bulk of Sespech's military.[10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p220-10) The most prominent troops of Sespech was Ormpetarr's cavalry, the second-best in the Vilhon Reach after [Lheshayl](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lheshayl "Lheshayl")'s.[22](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p55-22) The city guard were tasked with patrolling and assigned to stand sentry at Ormpetarr's many gates. Each member of the watch was outfitted with a polished [breastplate](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Breastplate "Breastplate"), adorned with the _[holy symbol](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Holy_symbol "Holy symbol")_ of [Helm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Helm "Helm"). Underneath, they wore a set of padded [leather armor](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Leather_armor "Leather armor") coats that hung low, ending around [human](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Human "Human") guard's knees. Their open-face [helmets](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Helmet "Helmet") were topped with purple plumes. City watch carried their weapons openly – knobbed [maces](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mace "Mace").[14](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-VipKis-ch4-14)

Between the _Spellplague_ and the Second Sundering, by [1479 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1479_DR "1479 DR"), there were several militias in town. The Order of Blue Flame had a militia known as the Peacekeepers, and Tyrangal had soldiers known as the [Copper Guard](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Copper_Guard "Copper Guard"). The Monastery of Kelemvor maintained a fighting force of [monks](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Monk "Monk") and [clerics](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Cleric "Cleric").[19](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TEoC-19)

## History

Ormpetarr was established long before the [Rotting War](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Rotting_War "Rotting War"), several years after the [Year of the Halfling's Dale, 135 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/135_DR "135 DR"). Quickly, it established itself as an important caravan stop for southern countries bringing their goods to the north. Ormpetarr invested the lion's share of its finances into establishing and maintaining the Golden Road, neglecting the defense budget. Due to the lack of defenses, the Mayor of Ormpetarr at the time began relying on Arrabar for defense. With years passing, Chondath rose to power and took over what would become the Barony of Sespech, which included Ormpetarr. For several centuries that followed, the Barony yearned to leave Chondath after growing dissatisfied with the rule.[1](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p27-1) By the advent of the Rotting War in the [Year of the Thirsty Sword, 900 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/900_DR "900 DR"), Ormpetarr has already attempted to secede over six times. The conflict ended in a disaster in [Year of the Queen's Tears, 902 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/902_DR "902 DR"), resulting in the collapse of Chondath, and Ormpetarr seized independence as a result.[³](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p10-3)

The late-14th century DR was tumultuous in Ormpetarr. An adventurer named Thuragar Foemaster rose to power in the [Year of the Wave, 1364 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1364_DR "1364 DR"), through a series of assassinations. He claimed the title of Baron for [Ricjolo Tomrase](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ricjolo_Tomrase "Ricjolo Tomrase"), a political puppet of Arrabar. Now Baron Foemaster drove his predecessor from Ormpetarr back to Chondath. In retaliation, Arrabar sent an army to [Arranford](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Arranford "Arranford") that stood roughly between the two cities. The Arrabarran "peace-keeping force" clashed with Baron Foemaster's men in a short battle from which Ormpetarr emerged victorious. Shortly after, Arranford was transformed into [Fort Arran](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Fort_Arran "Fort Arran") and housed 1,000 soldiers guarding Ormpetarr against its bitter neighbor. Despite his bloody rise to power, Baron Thuragar Foemaster had support from the citizens of Sespech, who approved of his anti-Chondath views.[12](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCG-p179-12) The Baron vowed to bring law to Ormpetarr, securing it from bandit caravan raids and the city's thieves' guild. The thieves staged bloody battles in the streets, attempting to rebel against the new Baron. The guildmaster at the time was [Haskar Corintis](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Haskar_Corintis "Haskar Corintis") whose disruptive tactics were sponsored by Chondath.[10](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCS3e-p220-10)

In the century that followed the calamity of the _[Spellplague](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Spellplague "Spellplague")_, Vilhon Reach was devastated by wild magics, becoming known as the [Vilhon Wilds](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Vilhon_Wilds "Vilhon Wilds"). By the [Year of the Ring, 1393 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1393_DR "1393 DR"),[[23]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-drag362-23) much of the city was full of half-collapsed and abandoned buildings.[[24]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-PoS-p196-24) When the border of the Plaguewrought Lands stabilized in the center of Ormpetarr, the city became a center for [Scar Pilgrimages](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Scar_Pilgrimage "Scar Pilgrimage") from whence travelers ventured into the rain Wilds in hopes of acquiring a _[spellscar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Spellscar "Spellscar")_.[19](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TEoC-19) The pilgrimages were inspired by [Madruen Morganoug](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Madruen_Morganoug "Madruen Morganoug") who accidentally became _spellscarred_ with skin aglow in blue fire. His skin absorbed the _Spellplague_ flames, making it almost impervious to [swords](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sword "Sword") and [daggers](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dagger "Dagger"). According to the locals, the success rate for the pilgrims looking to get _spellscarred_ was one out of every ten surviving individuals.[[25]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-PloSp-ch14-25) Significant number of pilgrims perished in the Vilhon Wilds left behind orphans who roamed Ormpetarr in gangs that labored by day and pickpocketed or thieved by night.[²](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRPG4e-p126-2)

With _Spellplague's_ end and the goddess of magic [Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra "Mystra")'s return during the Second Sundering that started in the [Year of the Narthex Murders, 1482 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1482_DR "1482 DR"), the lands of Vilhon Reach were returned to Toril, damage done to the world was healed, and wild magics of the [Plaguewrought Land](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Plaguewrought_Land "Plaguewrought Land") – wiped by the goddess's hand.[[26]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-EdTweet-26)

## Notable Locations

### Landmarks

- Foesmasher Keep, the seat of power in Ormpetarr, located at the center of the eastern edge of the city, surrounded by tall walls.[15](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p29-15)
- Nagapark, a large city park that stood just in front of the Foesmasher Keep walls.[15](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p29-15)
- The border of the Plaguewrought Land ran through the center of town and appeared as an eye-watering cloud of color. The Plaguewrought Land was healed in the Second Sundering, returning the city to its past size and safety.[16](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-PoS-p194-16)[19](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TEoC-19)[11](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCG-p193-11)
- Tyrangal's Mansion, a large mansion that belonged to the [copper dragon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Copper_dragon "Copper dragon") Tyrangal in the years of the _Spellplague_. The mansion stood alone, surrounded by collapsed buildings, and was heavily warded against intruders.[19](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TEoC-19)

### Inns & Taverns

- Finara's, a popular 15th century inn that catered to pilgrims and travelers like every other establishment in the city. [Finara](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Finara "Finara") was the inn's elderly owner, and she offered reasonably-priced accommodations.[11](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-FRCG-p193-11)
- Jewel, a filthy [beer](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Beer "Beer") and urine-smelling festhall and a brothel that existed in the century between the _Spellplague_ and the Second Sundering. Its most famous employee was [Moirah](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Moirah "Moirah") with her [spellscar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Spellscar "Spellscar") just below the navel.[19](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TEoC-19)

### Temples & Shrines

- High House of Swords and Banners, colloquially referred to as "the Bloodhall". The biggest temple dedicated to [Tempus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tempus "Tempus") that began as a meeting spot for mercenary companies of the Vilhon Reach several centuries before [1370 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1370_DR "1370 DR").[7](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-F&A-p159-7)
- Monastery of Kelemvor, built in [1479 DR](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/1479_DR "1479 DR") on the southern side of town, ministered to pilgrims undertaking their Scar Pilgrimage, most of whom died during the experience.[19](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TEoC-19)
- Temple of Silvanus, a large temple dedicated to the worship of the Oak Father.[6](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p17-6)
- Temple of Tyr, one of the biggest temples dedicated to [Tyr](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tyr "Tyr") in the Vilhon Reach.[8](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p20-8)
- Ormpetarr had several minor shrines dedicated to [Eldath](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Eldath "Eldath"), [Helm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Helm "Helm")[5](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ormpetarr#cite_note-TVR-p15-5)

## Ormpetarr: From Spellplague to Ruin
*[DnD Detective](https://dnddetective.com/author/dnddetective/), February 13, 2020*

A city that once boasted some 55,000 people was reduced to ruin after the Spellplague. Over 100 years later it’s never really bounced back.

### Background

Life in Ormpetarr (the capital of the former nation of Sespech) hasn’t always been easy. Sespech was a relatively small nation and as such had to worry about being annexed by neighbouring Chondath constantly. To protect itself it had to reach out to neighbouring city-states like Hlondeth for support, even when (in the case of Hlondeth) they are controlled by less than ideal allies (the Yuan-Ti).

When the spellplague hit the Vilhon Reach region was severely affected. The actual Vilhon Reach itself was drained of all its water, and Ormpetarr itself was split into two with the border of the [Plaguewrought Land](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Plaguewrought_Land) falling right down the middle of it. The devastation inflicted on the city also was calamitous to its population. It went from around 55,000 in 1372 DR to around 4,000 a hundred years later. The city was completely devastated by the spellplague and the years that followed. Even outside of the city earthmotes, swaths of moving rock, and great ravines that led directly to the Underdark would have been commonplace here around 1479 DR.

It’s not known what became of the region after the Second Sundering and the return of Chondath and Halruaa.

### What Makes it Unique Compared to Other Cities?

A hundred years ago, it was a typical walled city on water that you see in a lot of fantasy settings. While the city wasn’t unique, the nation of Sespech did have a few things unique about it. These could also be applied to smaller cities in Sespech like Elbulder and Mimph.

#### Pre-Spellplague

**Strict Magic Controls**

Anyone who uses magic to harm another in Sespech can expect a swift execution.

**Under Constant Threat of Invasion**

Chondath has had its eye on taking back this former barony. It’s likely sponsored bandits on the roads solely for the reason of de-legitimizing the rule of Sespech’s current ruler.

**Assassination Attempts on its Ruler are Common**

Not only does Chondath want to take back its former barony, but the thieves guild has never been happy about the situation in Sespech either. It’s on more than one occasion arranged assassinations against the leader of Sespech (paid for by Chondath).

**Hearings Held in Open Court**

Sespech is not a nation that does hearings behind closed doors. When it broke free from Chondath this is one of the things that the change in government introduced. This also makes the very open in terms of how justice is administered.

#### Post-Spellplague

Post spellplague it is said to be now a city of 4,000 people with no formal government. The [Order of Blue Fire](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_Blue_Flame) was the only organization that really offered any form of order in the city. Even then they were not interested in helping the city recover but instead actually sought to expand the plaguelands (that had split the city in two). The city was also a key attraction for pilgrims hoping to gain magic power through its [Festival of Blue Fire](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Festival_of_Blue_Fire).

With the events of the Second Sundering having happened it’s not really known what state Ormpetarr is in. It’s likely that it is still a frontier city although with spellscar no longer being a thing the Order of the Blue Fire likely disbanded. The city may now be under the authority of the copper dragon Tyrangal, or it may continue to be lawless.

### What are the City’s Issues?

**A City of Ruins**

You don’t go from being a city of 55,000 to 4,000 without leaving behind a fair amount of ruins. There should be a rich opportunity for any adventuring party here to go searching through these ruins in search of wealth and treasure.

**Lack of Any Government**

It’s not really clear at this point what has happened to the city since the Second Sundering. There is a good chance, however, that it still has no formal government. Without the Order of the Blue Fire keeping order, the city may have descended into becoming a haven for criminals.

**Leftovers From The Spellplague**

It could actually very well be that things like earthmotes (big floating chunks of earth) continue to be present north of the city. Other leftover effects of the spellplague could linger in the area around it.

### How Can I Use It in My Campaign?

Ormpetarr represents a city not only that lies in ruin, but that also has been deeply touched by some truly strange magic. I would play up this quirky angle if I were running the city myself.

For instance, maybe a new lord has taken over, and their manor is found on top of a floating earthmote. Maybe the city is now shaped around the crevasses and ridges created by the spellplague. Perhaps poor quality wooden bridges have been thrown together to connect what used to be a city with many different quarters.

Ultimately, I think this city works best if you play up the impact of the spellplague and make it out to be a place where unusual magic is still strong. Even having zones of wild magic or dead magic zones is an option.
