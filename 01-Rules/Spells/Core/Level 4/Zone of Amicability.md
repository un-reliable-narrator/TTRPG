# Zone of Amicability
[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [All Spells](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:all) » Zone of Amicability

Source: Arcana Unleashed

_Level 4 Enchantment (Bard, Warlock)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Self  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** 10 minutes

A magical zone of amicability radiates from you in a 60-foot Emanation for the duration. When you make an ability check to influence a creature in that area, you can treat a d20 roll of 9 or lower as a 10.
