# Skin Slough

5th-level necromancy

**Casting Time**: 1 action

**Range**: 120 feet

**Components**: V, S, M (a pinch of dried skin flakes)

**Duration**: Concentration, up to 1 minute

**Classes**: [cleric](https://www.5esrd.com/classes/cleric), [warlock](https://www.5esrd.com/classes/warlock), [wizard](https://www.5esrd.com/classes/wizard)

The target takes 3d8 necrotic damage each round, or half as much on a successful [Constitution](https://www.5esrd.com/using-ability-scores#TOC-Constitution) saving throw. Each time it fails a saving throw, it gains 1 level of [exhaustion](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/conditions/#Exhaustion) as its skin loosens and splits. When the target reaches 4 levels of [exhaustion](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/conditions/#Exhaustion), its skin tears apart and peels away, revealing its raw flesh and musculature underneath. The target ceases taking further damage or [exhaustion](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/conditions/#Exhaustion) from the spell, but it has disadvantage on saving throws against disease, poison, stunning, and any magical effect that specifically causes pain (not just hit point damage), and any [attack](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/combat#TOC-Attack) or effect that deals hit point damage other than mental damage deals an additional 1d6 damage to the target. The target also has disadvantage [Constitution](https://www.5esrd.com/using-ability-scores#TOC-Constitution) and [Charisma](https://www.5esrd.com/using-ability-scores#TOC-Charisma) checks and saving throws other than [Charisma](https://www.5esrd.com/using-ability-scores#TOC-Charisma) ([Intimidation](https://www.5esrd.com/using-ability-scores#Intimidation)) checks. All effects of skin slough are ended immediately by [regenerate](https://www.5esrd.com/database/spell/regenerate). If the target’s [exhaustion](https://www.5esrd.com/gamemastering/conditions/#Exhaustion) is reduced to zero, the other effects of skin slough can be removed with [greater restoration](https://www.5esrd.com/database/spell/greater-restoration) or one week of rest.
