Power Word Fortify

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [All Spells](all) » Power Word Fortify

Source: Player's Handbook

_Level 7 Enchantment (Bard, Cleric)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** 60 foot  
**Components:** V  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You fortify up to six creatures you can see within range. The spell bestows 120 Temporary Hit Points, which you divide among the spell’s recipients.
