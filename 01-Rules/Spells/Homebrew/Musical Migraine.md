# Musical Migraine
*Source: Critical Role Campaign 4, used by [Murray Mag'Nesson](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Murray_Mag%27Nesson "Murray Mag'Nesson")*

​*Level 4 Illusion (Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** 
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You hear faint echoes of music in your mind. You can hum fragments to unlock its subharmonic power while targeting one creature you can see within range. The target makes a constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they take 3d6 thunder damage and are incapacitated.
