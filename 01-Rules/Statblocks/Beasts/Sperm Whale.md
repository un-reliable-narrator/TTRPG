If the whale takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, the whale must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which fall [prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_phb) in a space within 10 feet of the whale.
