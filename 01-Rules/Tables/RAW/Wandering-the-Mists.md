# Wandering the Mists

So long as a Darklord hasn’t closed their domain’s borders, creatures that enter the Mists might travel to other domains. When characters without a Mist talisman enter the Mists surrounding a domain, they emerge elsewhere after 1d6 hours of travel. Roll on or take inspiration from the Wandering the Mists table to determine where the Mists relocate characters. 

| 1d100 | Characters Emerge from the Mists …                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01–20 | In a domain of your choosing.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 21–40 | After wandering for another 1d6 hours and rolling again on this table.                                                                                                                                            |
| 41–65 | 1d100 feet from where they entered.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 66–75 | A mile from where they entered at midnight of the following night.                                                                                                                                                |
| 76–85 | Inside a structure within the domain they tried to leave, perhaps a cave, crypt, shed, or closet.                                                                                                                 |
| 86–95 | After encountering creatures within the Mists. Roll any die. If you roll an even number, a terri«ed Commoner appears. If you roll an odd number, a Mist Horror (see chapter 5) appears. Roll again on this table. |
| 96–99 | Where they entered, only to find the domain changed. Perhaps someone the characters knew is gone and no one remembers the person.                                                                                 |
| 100   | On a world on the Material Plane. After 1d6 hours, the Mists rise around them once more and return them to the domain they departed.                                                                              |
