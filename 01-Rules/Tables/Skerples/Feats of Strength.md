*Some creatures may perform a Feat of Strength once per Long Rest (free action).*

Anyone can learn a Feat of Strength, but you only get one, chosen at random. To use it, you must have 19+ Strength, be raging, or succeed on a DC25 Strength (Athletics) check (*Failure expends your use)*.

| 1d10 | Feats of Strength                                                                                   |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | Instantly leap 20' in any direction, then make an attack against one target.                        |
| 2    | Headbutt. Deal 1d4+1 damage to a target. Target must Save or be stunned for 1 round.                |
| 3    | One shield or piece of armor on a target is destroyed. Magic items get a Save.                      |
| 4    | Heal fully and stand up from prone. Works even if dead, provided head is still attached.            |
| 5    | Target must Save or drop one weapon or item they are holding. It lands 10' away.                    |
| 6    | Hurl a weapon, object, or severed limb up to 100'. Does 1d6+1 damage on a hit.                      |
| 7    | Tear someone’s arms off. Make 2 attacks. If both hit, target takes 3d6+3 damage.                    |
| 8    | Scream so loud that hirelings, animals, and timid people within 50' must Save against fear or flee. |
| 9    | Next attack deals 1d8+2 damage. Even on a miss, target must Save or be knocked prone.               |
| 10   | Break something. Burst bonds, split chains, topple pillars, or annihilate doors.                    |
