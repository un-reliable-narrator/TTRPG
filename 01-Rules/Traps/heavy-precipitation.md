# Heavy Precipitation
*Environmental Hazard*  

Everything within an area of heavy rain or heavy snowfall is [Lightly Obscured](lightly-obscured.md), and creatures in the area have [Disadvantage](01-Rules/Rules/Disadvantage.md) on all Wisdom ([Perception](01-Rules/Rules/Skills.md#Perception)) checks. Heavy rain also extinguishes open flames.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 69*
