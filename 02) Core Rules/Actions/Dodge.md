If you take the Dodge action, you gain the following benefits: until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has [[02) Core Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] if you can see the attacker, and you make Dexterity saving throws with [[Advantage|Advantage]].

You lose these benefits if you have the [[Incapacitated]] condition or if your [[02) Core Rules/Rules/Speed|Speed]] is 0.
