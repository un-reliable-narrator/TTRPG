### Indomitable (Level 9)

If you fail a saving throw, you can reroll it with a bonus equal to your Fighter level. You must use the new roll, and you can't use this feature again until you finish a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md).

You can use this feature twice before a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md) starting at level 13 and three times before a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md) starting at level 17.
