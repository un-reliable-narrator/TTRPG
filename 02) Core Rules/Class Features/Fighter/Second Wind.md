### Second Wind (Level 1)

You have a limited well of physical and mental stamina that you can draw on. As a [Bonus Action](app://obsidian.md/bonus-action.md), you can use it to regain [Hit Points](app://obsidian.md/hit-points.md) equal to 10(1d10) plus your Fighter level.

You can use this feature twice. You regain one expended use when you finish a [Short Rest](app://obsidian.md/short-rest.md), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md).

When you reach certain Fighter levels, you gain more uses of this feature, as shown in the Second Wind column of the Fighter Features table.
