# Eldritch Smite
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 155*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5, [[pact-of-the-blade|pact of the blade]]

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can expend a Pact Magic spell slot to deal an extra  `dice: 1d8` Force damage to the target, plus another  `dice: 1d8` per level of the spell slot, and you can give the target the [Prone](Conditions.md#Prone) condition if it is Huge or smaller.
