# Unarmed Fighting
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 210*  

**Prerequisite**: Fighting Style Feature

When you hit with your [Unarmed Strike](Unarmed%20Strike.md) and deal damage, you can deal Bludgeoning damage equal to `dice: 1d6` plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of an [Unarmed Strike](Unarmed%20Strike.md). If you aren't holding any weapons or a [Shield](Shield) when you make the attack roll, the `dice: d6` becomes a `dice: d8`.

At the start of each of your turns, you can deal `dice: 1d4` Bludgeoning damage to one creature [Grappled](Conditions.md#Grappled) by you.
