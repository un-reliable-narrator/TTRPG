# Durable
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 203*  

**Prerequisite**: 4th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Defy Death.** You have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on [Death Saving Throws](death-saving-throw.md).

**Speedy Recovery.** As a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md), you can expend one of your [Hit Point Dice](hit-point-dice.md), roll the die, and regain a number of [Hit Points](hit-points.md) equal to the roll.
