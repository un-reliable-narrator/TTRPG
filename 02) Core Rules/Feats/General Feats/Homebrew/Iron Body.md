# Iron Body
*Feat*

*When flexed, your muscles are stronger than steel.* 

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Your Strength increases by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Steel Yourself.** When you'd take bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage from an attack roll, as a reaction, you can reduce the damage by 1d10 + Str mod.
