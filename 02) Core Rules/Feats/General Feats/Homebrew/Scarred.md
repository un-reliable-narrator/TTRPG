# Scarred
*Feat*

**Prerequisite:** 
**Trigger:** You've been reduced to 0 HP by slashing damage.[^1]

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Is that all you've got?** You gain resistance to Slashing damage.

**Gruesome Sight.** Gain advantage on Intimidation checks when you show off your scars.
