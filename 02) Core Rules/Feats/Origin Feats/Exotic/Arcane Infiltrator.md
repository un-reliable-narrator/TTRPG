# Arcane Infiltrator
_Origin Feat_

You gain the following benefits.

_**Cantrip.**_ You learn the [Friends](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618924-friends) cantrip. Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell (choose when you select this feat).

_**Cunning Diversion.**_ You can take the [Dodge](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#DodgeAction) action as a Bonus Action. You can use this benefit a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.


Source: *Dungeon Masters: Living Spells Play-Along Pack*
