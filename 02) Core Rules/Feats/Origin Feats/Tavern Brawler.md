# Tavern Brawler
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 202*  

You gain the following benefits.

**Enhanced Unarmed Strike.** When you hit with your [Unarmed Strike](Unarmed%20Strike.md) and deal damage, you can deal Bludgeoning damage equal to `dice: 1d4` plus your Strength modifier instead of the normal damage of an [Unarmed Strike](Unarmed%20Strike.md).

**Damage Rerolls.** Whenever you roll a damage die for your [Unarmed Strike](Unarmed%20Strike.md), you can reroll the die if it rolls a 1, and you must use the new roll.

**Improvised Weaponry.** You have proficiency with improvised weapons.

**Push.** When you hit a creature with an [Unarmed Strike](Unarmed%20Strike.md) as part of the [[Attack]] action on your turn, you can deal damage to the target and also push it 5 feet away from you. You can use this benefit only once per turn.
