# Healer's Kit
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 5 gp
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

A Healer's Kit has ten uses. As a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action, you can expend one of its uses to stabilize an [Unconscious](Conditions.md#Unconscious) creature that has 0 [Hit Points](hit-points.md) without needing to make a Wisdom ([Medicine](Medicine.md)) check.

*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 225. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
