####  Potion of Healing (Greater)
---
*Potion, uncommon*

You regain 4d4 + 4 [Hit Points](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#HitPoints) when you drink this potion.

Whatever its potency, the potion’s red liquid glimmers when agitated.

*Notes: Bonus: Hit Points, Healing, Consumable*
