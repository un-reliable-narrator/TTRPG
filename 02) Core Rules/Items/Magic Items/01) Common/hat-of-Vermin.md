# Hat of Vermin
*Wondrous item, common*  
![](items/XDMG/Hat of Vermin.webp#right)


This hat has 3 charges. While holding the hat, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to expend 1 charge and summon your choice of a [Bat](Bat.md), a [frog](frog.md), or a [Rat](Rat.md). The summoned creature magically appears in the hat and tries to get away from you as quickly as possible. The creature is [Indifferent](indifferent-attitude.md) toward you and other creatures, and it isn't under your control. It behaves as an ordinary creature of its kind and disappears after 1 hour or when it drops to 0 [Hit Points](hit-points.md). The hat regains all expended charges daily at dawn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 267*
