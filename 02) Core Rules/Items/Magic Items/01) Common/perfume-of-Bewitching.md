# Perfume of Bewitching
*Wondrous item, common*  



This tiny vial contains magic perfume, enough for one use. You can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to apply the perfume to yourself, and its effect lasts 1 hour. For the duration, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on all Charisma ([Deception](Deception.md) and [Persuasion]) checks made to influence a creature within 5 feet of yourself.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 284*
