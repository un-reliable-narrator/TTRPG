# Potion of Climbing
*Potion, common*  
![](items/XDMG/Potion of Climbing.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, you gain a [Climb Speed](climb-speed.md) equal to your [Speed](Speed.md) for 1 hour. During this time, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Strength ([Athletics](Athletics.md)) checks to climb.

This potion is separated into brown, silver, and gray layers resembling bands of stone. Shaking the bottle fails to mix the colors.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 287. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
