# Prosthetic Limb
*Wondrous item, common*  
![](items/XDMG/Prosthetic Limb.webp#right)


This magic item replaces a lost limb—a hand, an arm, a foot, a leg, or a similar body part. While the prosthetic is attached, it functions identically to the part it replaces. You can detach or reattach it as a [Magic](Magic.md) action, and it can't be removed against your will while you are alive.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 290*
