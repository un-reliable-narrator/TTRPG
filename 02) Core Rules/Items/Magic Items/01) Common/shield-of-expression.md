# Shield of Expression
*Armor ([Shield](Shield)), common*  


- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

The front of this Shield is shaped in the likeness of a face. While bearing the Shield, you can take a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md) to alter the face's expression.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 303*
