# Lantern of Revealing
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![](items/XDMG/Lantern of Revealing.webp#right)


While lit, this hooded lantern burns for 6 hours on 1 pint of oil, shedding [Bright Light](bright-light.md) in a 30-foot radius and [Dim Light](dim-light.md) for an additional 30 feet. [Invisible](Conditions.md#Invisible) creatures and objects are visible as long as they are in the lantern's [Bright Light](bright-light.md). You can take a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action to lower the hood, reducing the lantern's light to [Dim Light](dim-light.md) in a 5-foot radius.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 275. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
