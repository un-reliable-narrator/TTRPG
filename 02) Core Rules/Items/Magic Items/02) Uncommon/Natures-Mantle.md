# Nature's Mantle
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a druid or ranger)*  
![118](Natures-Mantle.webp#right)


This cloak shifts color and texture to blend with the terrain surrounding you. While wearing the cloak, you can use it as a [Spellcasting Focus](spellcasting-focus.md) for your Druid and Ranger spells.

While you are in an area that is [Lightly Obscured](lightly-obscured.md), you can [Hide](Hide.md) as a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md) even if you are being directly observed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 280*
