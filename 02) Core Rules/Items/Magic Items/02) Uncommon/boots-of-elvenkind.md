# Boots of Elvenkind
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![](items/XDMG/Boots of Elvenkind.webp#right)


While you wear these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you are moving across. You also have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Dexterity ([Stealth](Stealth.md)) checks.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 239. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
