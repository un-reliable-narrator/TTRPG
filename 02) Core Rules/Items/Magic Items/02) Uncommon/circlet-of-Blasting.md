# Circlet of Blasting
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  


While wearing this circlet, you can cast [Scorching Ray](Scorching-Ray.md) with it (`+5` to hit). The circlet can't cast this spell again until the next dawn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
