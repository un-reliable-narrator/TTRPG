# Cloak of Elvenkind
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Cloak of Elvenkind.webp#right)


While you wear this cloak, Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) checks made to perceive you have [[02) Core Rules/Rules/Disadvantage|Disadvantage]], and you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Dexterity ([Stealth](Stealth.md)) checks.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
