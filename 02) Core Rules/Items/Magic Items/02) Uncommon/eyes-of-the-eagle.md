# Eyes of the Eagle
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  


These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) checks that rely on sight. In conditions of clear visibility, you can make out details of even extremely distant creatures and objects as small as 2 feet across.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 261. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
