# Keoghtom's Ointment
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![](items/XDMG/Keoghtoms Ointment.webp#right)


This glass jar, 3 inches in diameter, contains `dice: 1d4 + 1` doses of a thick mixture that smells faintly of aloe. The jar and its contents weigh 1/2 pound.

As a [Utilize](Utilize.md) action, you can swallow one dose of the ointment or apply it to a creature within 5 feet of yourself. The creature that receives it regains `2d8 + 2` [Hit Points](hit-points.md) and ceases to have the [Poisoned](Conditions.md#Poisoned) condition.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 275. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> (as "Restorative Ointment")*
