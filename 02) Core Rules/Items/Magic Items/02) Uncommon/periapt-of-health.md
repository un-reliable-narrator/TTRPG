# Periapt of Health
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Periapt of Health.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

While wearing this pendant, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to regain `2d4 + 2` [Hit Points](hit-points.md). Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

In addition, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on saving throws to avoid or end the [Poisoned](Conditions.md#Poisoned) condition while you wear this pendant.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 284. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
