# Winged Boots
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Winged Boots.webp#right)


These boots have 4 charges and regain `dice: 1d4` expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the boots, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to expend 1 charge, gaining a [Fly Speed](fly-speed.md) of 30 feet for 1 hour. If you are flying when the duration expires, you descend at a rate of 30 feet per round until you land.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 325. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
