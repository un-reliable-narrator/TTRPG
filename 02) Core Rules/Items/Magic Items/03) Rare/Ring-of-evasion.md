# Ring of Evasion
*Ring, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Ring of Evasion.webp#right)


This ring has 3 charges, and it regains `dice: 1d3` expended charges daily at dawn. When you fail a Dexterity saving throw while wearing the ring, you can take a [Reaction](reaction.md) to expend 1 charge to succeed on that save instead.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
