# Cape of the Mountebank
*Wondrous item, rare*  
![](items/XDMG/Cape of the Mountebank.webp#right)


This cape smells faintly of brimstone. While wearing it, you can use it to cast [Dimension Door](Dimension-Door.md) as a [Magic](Magic.md) action. This property can't be used again until the next dawn.

When you teleport with that spell, you leave behind a cloud of smoke. The space you left is [Lightly Obscured](lightly-obscured.md) by that smoke until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 242. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
