# Quaal's Feather Token, Bird
*Wondrous item, rare*  
![](items/XDMG/Quaals Feather Token (Bird).webp#right)


This object looks like a feather.

You can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to toss the token 5 feet into the air. The token disappears and an enormous, multicolored bird takes its place. The bird has the statistics of a [roc](roc.md), but it can't attack. It obeys your simple commands and can carry up to 500 pounds while flying at its maximum speed (16 miles per hour for a maximum of 144 miles per day, with a 1-hour rest for every 3 hours of flying) or 1,000 pounds at half that speed. The bird disappears after flying its maximum distance for a day or if it drops to 0 [Hit Points](hit-points.md). You can dismiss the bird as a [Magic](Magic.md) action.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 290. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> (as "Feather Token, Bird") and the Free Rules (2024)*
