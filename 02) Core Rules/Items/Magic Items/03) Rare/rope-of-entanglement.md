# Rope of Entanglement
*Wondrous item, rare*  
![](items/XDMG/Rope of Entanglement.webp#right)


This rope is 30 feet long. While holding one end of the rope, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to command the other end to dart forward and entangle one creature you can see within 20 feet of yourself. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or have the [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) condition. You can release the target by letting go of your end of the rope (causing the rope to coil up in the target's space) or by using a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md) to repeat the command (causing the rope to coil up in your hand).

A target [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) by the rope can take an action to make its choice of a DC 15 Strength ([Athletics](Athletics.md)) or Dexterity ([Acrobatics](Acrobatics.md)) check. On a successful check, the target is no longer [Restrained](Conditions.md#Restrained) by the rope. If you're still holding onto the rope when a target escapes from it, you can take a [Reaction](reaction.md) to command the rope to coil up in your hand; otherwise, the rope coils up in the target's space.

The rope has AC 20, HP 20, and [Immunity](Immunity.md) to Poison and Psychic damage. It regains 1 [Hit Point](hit-points.md) every 5 minutes as long as it has at least 1 [Hit Point](hit-points.md). If the rope drops to 0 [Hit Points](hit-points.md), it is destroyed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 301. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
