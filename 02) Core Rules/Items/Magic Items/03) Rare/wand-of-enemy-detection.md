# Wand of Enemy Detection
*Wand, rare (requires attunement)*  
![](items/XDMG/Wand of Enemy Detection.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can take a [Magic](Magic.md) action to expend 1 charge. For 1 minute, you know the direction of the nearest creature [Hostile](hostile-attitude.md) to you within 60 feet, but not its distance from you. The wand can sense the presence of [Hostile](hostile-attitude.md) creatures that are [Invisible](Conditions.md#Invisible), ethereal, disguised, or hidden, as well as those in plain sight. The effect ends if you stop holding the wand.

## Regaining Charges

The wand regains `dice: 1d6 + 1` expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll `dice: 1d20`. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 319. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
