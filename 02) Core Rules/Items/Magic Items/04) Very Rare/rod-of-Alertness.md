# Rod of Alertness
*Rod, very rare (requires attunement)*  


- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

This rod has the following properties.

## Alertness

While holding the rod, you have [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) checks and on [Initiative](Initiative.md) rolls. Spells. While holding the rod, you can cast the following spells from it:

- [Detect Evil and Good](Detect-Evil-and-Good.md)  
- [Detect Dagic](Detect-Magic.md)  
- [Detect Poison and Disease](Detect-Poison-and-Disease.md)  
- [See Invisibility](See-Invisibility.md)  

## Protective Aura

As a [Magic](Magic.md) action, you can plant the haft end of the rod in the ground, whereupon the rod's head sheds [Bright Light](bright-light.md) in a 60-foot radius and [Dim Light](dim-light.md) for an additional 60 feet. While in that [Bright Light](bright-light.md), you and your allies gain a +1 bonus to [Armor Class](Armor%20Class.md) and saving throws and can sense the location of any [Invisible](Conditions.md#Invisible) creature that is also in the [Bright Light](bright-light.md).

The rod's head stops glowing and the effect ends after 10 minutes or when a creature takes a [Magic](Magic.md) action to pull the rod from the ground. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 299. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
