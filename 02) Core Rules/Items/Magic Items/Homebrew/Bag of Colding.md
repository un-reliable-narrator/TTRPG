# Bag of Colding
*Wondrous Item, uncommon*

This item resembles a bag of holding, but is cool to the touch and emits visibly cold air while opened. It can only hold 100 pounds of items, but any perishable items do not degrade while within the bag. It is otherwise identical to a [bag of holding](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4581-bag-of-holding).

Notes: Utility, Container
