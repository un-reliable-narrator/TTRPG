# Ring of the Grammarian
*Ring, rare*

The wearer of this ring can, once a day, alter one letter of a spell's title to alter the spell for a different effect.

Example: _Cause Fear_ becomes _Cause Bear_.

The effect of these altered spells are determined by the DM, but with the caster's intentions taken into account.
