# Evasive Footwork
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 95*  

As a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md), you can expend one Superiority Die and take the [Disengage](03)%20Resources/Tables/RAW/Actions.md#Disengage) action. You also roll the die and add the number rolled to your AC until the start of your next turn.
