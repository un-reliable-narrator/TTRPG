# Gift of the Depths
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 156*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 5

You can breathe underwater, and you gain a [Swim Speed](swim-speed.md) equal to your [Speed](Speed.md).

You can also cast [Water Breathing](Water-Breathing.md) once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it in this way again when you finish a [Long Rest](long-rest.md).
