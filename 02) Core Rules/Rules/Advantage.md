# Advantage
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 360. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

If you have Advantage on a [D20 Test](d20-test.md), roll two d20s, and use the higher roll. A roll can't be affected by more than one Advantage, and Advantage and [[variant-rules/disadvantage|Disadvantage]]
