# Passive Perception
*Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 372. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)* 

Passive Perception is a score that reflects a creature's general awareness of its surroundings. The DM uses this score when determining whether a creature notices something without consciously making a Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) check.

A creature's Passive Perception equals 10 plus the creature's Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) check bonus. If the creature has [[Advantage|Advantage]] on such checks, increase the score by 5. If the creature has [[02) Core Rules/Rules/variant-rules/disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on them, decrease the score by 5. For example, a level 1 character with a Wisdom of 15 and proficiency in Perception has a Passive Perception of 14 (`10 + 2 + 2`). If that character has [[Advantage|Advantage]] on Wisdom ([Perception](Perception.md)) checks, the score becomes 19 (`10 + 2 + 2 + 5`).
