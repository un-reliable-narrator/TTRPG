Tortoise Shell

[Home](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/home:home) » [All Spells](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:all) » Tortoise Shell

Source: D&D Beyond Drops - June 2026

_Level 2 Abjuration (Artificer, Druid, Ranger)_

**Casting Time:** Action  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a willing creature. Until the spell ends, the target’s skin hardens into a tortoise shell, and the target gains a +3 bonus to AC. If the target moves, the bonus becomes +1 instead until the start of its next turn.
