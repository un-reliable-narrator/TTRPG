**AC** 18 ([chain mail](https://5e.tools/items.html#chain%20mail_phb), [shield](https://5e.tools/items.html#shield_phb))
**Initiative** +1 (11)
**HP** 16 (3d8 + 3)

### Traits
Formation Tactics. The soldier has advantage on saving throws against being [charmed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#charmed_phb), [frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_phb), [grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_phb), or [restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_phb) while it is within 5 feet of at least one ally.

### Actions

***Multiattack***. The soldier makes two melee attacks.

***Longsword***. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage, or 7 (1d10 + 2) slashing damage if used with two hands.
