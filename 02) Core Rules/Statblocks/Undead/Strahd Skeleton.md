# Strahd Skeleton
_Medium Undead, Lawful Evil_
[RHW](https://5e.tools/book.html#RHW,page:269 "Ravenloft: The Horrors Within")[p269](https://5e.tools/book.html#RHW,page:269 "Page 269")

**AC** 16
**Initiative** +1 (11)
**HP** 65 (10d8 + 20)
**Speed** 30 ft.

 **Str**  17   +3   +5   **Dex**  13   +1   +1   **Con**  15   +2   +2  
 **Int**  12   +1   +1   **Wis**  15   +2   +2   **Cha**  16   +3   +3  

 **Vulnerabilities** Bludgeoning
 **Immunities** Poison; [Exhaustion](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#exhaustion_phb), [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_phb), [Poisoned](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#poisoned_phb) 
 **Gear** [Longsword](https://5e.tools/items.html#longsword_xphb), [Shield](https://5e.tools/items.html#shield_xphb)
 **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 12
 **Languages** Understands Common but can't speak   
 **CR** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2) 
 
### Traits
  
**Undead Fortitude**. If damage reduces the skeleton to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), it makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken) unless the damage is Radiant or from a [Critical Hit](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#critical%20hit_xphb). On a successful save, the skeleton drops to 1 [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) instead.

### Actions

**Multiattack**. The skeleton makes two Vampiric Longsword attacks. It can replace one attack with a use of Shield Bash.

**Vampiric Longsword**. _Melee Attack Roll:_ +5, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) Slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) Necrotic damage. The target's [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken.

**Shield Bash**. _Strength Saving Throw:_ DC 13, one creature within 5 feet that the skeleton can see. _Failure:_ 10 (2d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it has the [Prone](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#prone_xphb) condition

#### Original Thoughts
**Strahd's Royal Guard.** At first glance, Strahd Skeletons may fill a similar thematic niche as the [[Wight]]s serving as former guards of Castle Ravenloft. Crucially, however, they do not speak. Also, in spite of a higher CR, they are arguably weaker in many respects. As such, they are more suited as enforcers and personal guards. 

**Skeletal Rider.** May appear mounted on a [[Warhorse-Skeleton|Warhorse Skeleton]] and benefit from the [[Mounted Combatant]] feat. (While mounted, has advantage attacking unmounted medium or smaller creatures, can force attacks targeting the warhorse to hit it instead, warhorse benefits from pseudo-Evasion while mounted.) While mounted, shield bash is re-flavored as charge or the horse kicking. If the rider is unmounted, incapacitated or destroyed, the warhorse may fight on.
