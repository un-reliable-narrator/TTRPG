### Traits

**Darklord Restoration**. If Strahd dies, he revives with all his [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) in 1d10 weeks somewhere in Barovia.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day, or 4/Day in Domain)**. If Strahd fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Misty Escape**. If Strahd drops to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) outside his resting place, he uses Shape-Shift to become mist (no action required). If he can't use Shape-Shift, he is destroyed. While Strahd has 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) in mist form, he can't return to his vampire form, and he must reach his resting place within 2 hours or be destroyed. Once in his resting place, Strahd returns to his vampire form and has the [Paralyzed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#paralyzed_xphb) condition until he regains any [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb), and he regains 1 [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) after spending 1 hour there.

**Spider Climb**. Strahd can climb difficult surfaces, including ceilings, without making an ability check.

**Vampire Weakness**. Strahd has these weaknesses:
- Forbiddance. Strahd can't enter a residence without an invitation from an occupant.
- Running Water. Strahd takes 20 Acid damage if he ends his turn in running water
- Stake to the Heart. If a weapon that deals Piercing damage is driven into Strahd's heart while Strahd has the [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb) condition in his resting place, Strahd has the [Paralyzed](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#paralyzed_xphb) condition until the weapon is removed.
- Sunlight. Strahd takes 20 Radiant damage if he starts his turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, he has [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb) on attack rolls and ability checks.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Strahd makes two Death Strike attacks. He can replace one of these attacks with a use of (A) Bite or (B) Spellcasting to cast [Fireball](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fireball_xphb) if available.

**Death Strike.** _Melee Attack Roll:_ +9, reach 5 ft. _Hit:_ 8 (1d8 + 4) Slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb) condition (escape DC 14) from one of two hands.

**Bite (Bat or Vampire Form Only).** _Constitution Saving Throw:_ DC 17, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or that has the [Grappled](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#grappled_xphb), [Incapacitated](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#incapacitated_xphb), or [Restrained](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#restrained_xphb) condition. _Failure:_ 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) Necrotic damage. The target's [Hit Point](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and Strahd regains [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) equal to that amount. A Humanoid reduced to 0 [Hit Points](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#hit%20points_xphb) by this damage rises the following night as a Vampire Spawn under Strahd's control, unless the Humanoid is restored to life or its body is destroyed.

Spellcasting. Strahd casts one of the following spells, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 18):

- At will: [Detect Thoughts](https://5e.tools/spells.html#detect%20thoughts_xphb), [Fog Cloud](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fog%20cloud_xphb), [Mage Hand](https://5e.tools/spells.html#mage%20hand_xphb)
    
- 2/day each: [Animate Dead](https://5e.tools/spells.html#animate%20dead_xphb), [Fireball](https://5e.tools/spells.html#fireball_xphb), [Gust of Wind](https://5e.tools/spells.html#gust%20of%20wind_xphb), [Mirror Image](https://5e.tools/spells.html#mirror%20image_xphb), [Nondetection](https://5e.tools/spells.html#nondetection_xphb)
    
- 1/day each: [Greater Invisibility](https://5e.tools/spells.html#greater%20invisibility_xphb), [Polymorph](https://5e.tools/spells.html#polymorph_xphb), [Scrying](https://5e.tools/spells.html#scrying_xphb)

### Bonus Actions

**Charm**. Strahd casts [Charm Person](https://5e.tools/spells.html#charm%20person_xphb), requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting. When the spell is cast this way, its duration is 24 hours, and the Charmed target is a willing recipient of Strahd's Bite, the damage of which doesn't end the spell. When the spell ends, the target is unaware it was Charmed by Strahd.

**Shape-Shift**. If Strahd isn't in sunlight or running water, he shape-shifts into a Tiny bat ([Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) 5 ft., [Fly Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#fly%20speed_xphb) 30 ft.), a Medium wolf ([Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) 40 ft.), or a Medium cloud of mist ([Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb) 5 ft., [Fly Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#fly%20speed_xphb) 20 ft. [hover]), or he returns to his vampire form. Anything he is wearing transforms with him.

While in bat or wolf form, Strahd can't speak. His game statistics, other than his size and [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb), are unchanged.

While in mist form, Strahd can't take any actions, speak, or manipulate objects. He is weightless and can enter an enemy's space and stop there. If air can pass through a space, Strahd can do so, but he can't pass through liquid. Strahd has [Resistance](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#resistance_xphb) to all damage, except the damage he takes from sunlight.

### Legendary Actions

_Legendary Action Uses: 3. Immediately after another creature's turn, Strahd can expend a use to take one of the following actions. Strahd regains all expended uses at the start of each of their turns._
- Count's Command. Strahd casts [Command](https://5e.tools/spells.html#command_xphb) (level 2 version), requiring no spell components and using the same spellcasting ability as Spellcasting.

- Striding Strike. Strahd moves up to half his [Speed](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#speed_xphb), and he makes one Death Strike attack.

### Regional Effects
Barovia is altered by Strahd's presence, creating the following effects:

- **Closing the Borders.** While in his domain, Strahd can close or open the borders to Barovia at will. While the borders are closed, noxious fog suffuses the Mists surrounding Barovia. A creature that enters the Mists for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 [Exhaustion](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#exhaustion_xphb) level. A creature makes this save only once per turn.

- **Dreadful Darkness.** While in an area of [Darkness](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#darkness_xphb), creatures other than Strahd and creatures of his choice have [Disadvantage](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#disadvantage_xphb) on saving throws to end or avoid the [Frightened](https://5e.tools/conditionsdiseases.html#frightened_xphb) condition.

- **Jealous Heart.** When a character receives [Heroic Inspiration](https://5e.tools/variantrules.html#heroic%20inspiration_xphb) but already has it, that character can't give it to another character in the group.

If Strahd dies, these effects end immediately.
