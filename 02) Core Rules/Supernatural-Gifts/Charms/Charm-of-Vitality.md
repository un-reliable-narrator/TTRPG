# Charm of Vitality

This Charm allows you to give yourself the benefit of a [Potion of Vitality](potion-of-Vitality.md) as a [Magic](Magic.md) action. Once you do so, the Charm vanishes from you.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99*
