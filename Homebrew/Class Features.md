###### Ocean Monk — Way of the Vast
fishing rod mundane item part of a fishing tackle, may be used for "foraging" if a suitable body of water is nearby. As an action, you may utilize the fishing rod to attempt to hook an object within 40 ft. that is at least one size category smaller than you and reel it in. Make a dexterity (sleight of hand) check (DC 15). If the object is being held by a creature who can see you, that creature may use their reaction to make a Strength save (DC equals 10 + your Dex mod). On a success, they keep their grip on the item. (You still reel in the hook.) fishing rod exotic weapon damage: 1 piercing range 30/90 ft. Vex Loading (Reeling in the line) On a critical hit, choose: - Pluck an eye: If target relies on eyes for sight, they become blinded. If they still have at least one more eye, the blindness ends on the start of your next turn. - Go for a soft spot: Deal an additional 1d6 damage. - 
**_Yoink!_** Instead of dealing damage, obtain an object the target is holding. (Per DM discretion, you may also steal certain worn or carried items, e.g. a hat, a badge.) 
monk subclass way of the vast you are proficient with fishing rods and they count as monk weapons for you. whenever you land a successful attack with a fishing rod, you may force the defender to make a strength save (dc equal to your grapple escape dc). on a failure, you may choose to: **_Reel._** Force the target to move 10 ft. toward you. **_Catch._**
