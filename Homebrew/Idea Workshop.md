# Ideas Workshop

##### Militia Training
- NPCs may "train" to upgrade their statblock to an eligible stronger one
This requires:
- Time: 10 (3d6) days, multiplied by the target CR (min. 1).
- Money: The price of the target statblock equipment, wages, food and lodging multiplied by the number of days. 
- Tutor(s) with the relevant skills, and possibly further wages.
	- 1on1 training: A creature may function as a tutor for it's own statblock for 1 pupil at a time.
	- Group training: A creature may tutor for a statblock it previously held and has since upgraded from for groups of 6 (2d6) at a time.
	- Class training: A creature may tutor for a statblock it previously held for classes of 22 (4d10) students at a time, if there are appropriate facilities in place. Double the training time required.

###### Humanoid Upgrade Paths:
Commoner
- Acolyte
- Apprentice Wizard
- Bandit
- Cultist
- Guard
- Performer
- Sage
- Scout
- Soldier
- Tough
- Warrior Infantry
