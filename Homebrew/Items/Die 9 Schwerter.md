# "CRUSADER"
Examples: [Heroic Resolve (tvtropes)](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/HeroicResolve)
#### Steely Resolve
Req: ~~2+ lv Martial~~ 1d8 or better HD
Until your turn ends, you don't suffer any effects from dropping to 0 HP.
#### Furious Counterstrike
Req: Steely Resolve
After you take damage, add +1 to your attack rolls and damage until the end of your turn. This bonus stacks up to +6 for each instance of damage taken.
#### Indomitable Soul
You add your CHA bonus on WIS saves.
#### Zealous Surge
1/day, reaction: Gain advantage on a save.
#### Mettle
On a successful WIS save, you may ignore the effect that caused it (instead of halving the damage).

#### Duel of Wills
Immediately before initiative is rolled for either side, you can instigate a duel of wills by selecting a single enemy you are aware of within 30 feet. Only characters with Intelligence scores of 3 or higher can engage in a duel of wills, and the participants must be able to see one another. You can’t attempt more than one duel of wills per encounter.
Both you and your chosen foe must make an Intimidate check. The character with the higher result gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls and damage rolls against the loser, and the loser takes a -2 penalty on attack rolls and damage rolls against the winner. On a tie, neither character gains any benefit. These bonuses and penalties last for the encounter.
