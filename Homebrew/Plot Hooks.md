## *Unstoppable Army*
*Inspired by [this animation](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EeHtXfVRHXI)*

**Scope:** Campaign.
**Themes:** War, indoctrination, collateral damage.

An army is on the loose. It has killed a king and toppled a nation. It is strange and unknowable. Rumors are numerous and wildly unreliable. Regents feel personally threatened. Armies are assembled across all nations to ward off the threat. Every battle ends in defeat. The army is powerful, but not large, and there is just one. It's soldiers are not invincible, but conventional warfare cannot defeat them at scale.

The unstoppable army carves an unpredictable path from battle to battle, taking castles and crowns along the way. Even so, it presents little threat in the grand scheme to the general civilian population. Devastation and collateral damage is instead caused by the many armies hastily raised wasted to defend against the unstoppable army, the raised taxes and emergency policies, the unrest, the deserters, the opportunists, etc. 

In other words: The actual threat is negligible and mostly confined to ruling monarchs and the elite's comfort or power structures. The true damage is caused by the fearmongering responses to the threat.


## *"Threat Level: Tarrasque"*
*A "Tarrasque-style" event should have: 
- A Sudden and Shocking Emergence
- A desperate emergency meeting of elite factions abandoning individual schemes in the face of apocalypse
- A sense of doom and the inevitability of great, tragic loss
- Immediate and uncompromising carnage
- Terrifying helplessness through failing plans and preparations
- Local Security Forces attempt containment. Their failure is decisive, swift and probably gory.
- Cease-all-activities-and-help-or-run broadcast "Calling on all Adventurers"
*Maybe also:*
- No connection to existing enemies or plots, violently disruption existing arcs, possibly a new status quo
- An Aftermath, a campaign can be just dealing with the fallout
*Inspirations: Worm's Endbringers, Godzilla*
*The Origin Monster of a Tarrasque-Event should be*:
- Bigger than Gargantuan. (Consider a new size category: *Vast*. Loosely defined as "too big for any single battlemap." Probably not a cube, probably has individually targeted limbs, segments.)
- (Blindly) Destructive, no (discernible) complex goal.
- Possibly Immortal. "Victory" is limiting civilian casualties or completing conditions to banish/redirect it.
- Causing environmental, passive harm
- Contain/constitute a living dungeon
