# Homebrew Rules

###### Spell Scrolls
*Spell scrolls can be used by anyone, not just those who know the inscribed spell.*
To cast a spell you don't know from a scroll, make an Intelligence ([[Arcana]]) check. The DC is equal to 10+ the spell's level. *Failure:* You fail to cast the spell. The scroll is not consumed.
*Natural 1:* The spell scroll is consumed. Roll on the [[Wild-Magic-Surge|Wild Magic Surge]] table (or comparable).
(To gain proficiency in the Arcana skill, spend ~1 year studying with access to a sufficiently arcane library, or take ~1 month of tutoring from a sufficiently advanced wizard. Or pay a mind sculptor.)
