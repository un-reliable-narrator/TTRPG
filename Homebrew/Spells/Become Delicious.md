# Become Delicious
*Level 1 Transmutation (Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Self
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** 1 minute

You appear irresistibly tasty. Other creatures within 30 ft. have disadvantage on concentration and wisdom saves, provided they can see or smell you. Hostile creatures may focus their attention on you, especially if they are currently motivated by hunger.

Does not affect Constructs and Celestials.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** If you cast this spell using a slot of level 5 or higher, the duration becomes permanent.
