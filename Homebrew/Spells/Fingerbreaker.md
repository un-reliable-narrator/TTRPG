# Fingerbreaker
*Level 1 Necromancy (Bard, Cleric, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 30 feet
**Components:** V, S
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You compel the bones within a creature's extremities to crack and contort. 
*Strength Save:* Target creature you can see. *Failure:* 2d6 necrotic damage and the target must drop one object it is holding, if anything. *Success:* Half damage only.

Does not affect creatures without bones or extremities. Can affect Constructs, but only those constructed from bone.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The damage increases by 1d6 for each added level.

*Trivia:*
- *Originally invented by the renowned ancient healing Mage Asklepios in a fit of rage.*
- *Once considered part of the now-discarded Ossomancy school, which contained spells to manipulate bone.*
