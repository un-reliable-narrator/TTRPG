###### The Merchant
Tags: *Recurring, Noncombat, NPC*
Encountered once on every floor. Sells a random assortment of Magic Items at 50% market price (items are tagged), as well as some original items and most adventuring gear, including 1d4 rations per party member.
Appears as an elderly male elf in light tunics. His shop is comfortably fitted with carpet and pillows, as well as several decorative masks. He does not permit anyone to stay for a Long Rest (for their own protection). Wandering Monsters can enter, but usually avoid combat with the Merchant (who does not step in). 
If attacked, uses the [[Archmage|Archmage]] statblock, with a [[Teleport]] spell rigged to transport him (but not his merchandise) away on defeat. The next shop appears as normal, except the Merchant is seemingly replaced by a polite letter carefully detailing the sum owed for the items stolen, with a small "Undankbarkeit"-fee added and the firm warning that this is a one-time offer of reconciliation. Items can be returned to substitute their price. The Merchant is present, though hidden by an Invisibility spell, and will attack any thief who attempts to leave without the full dept paid.
If reconciliation is not achieved, subsequent shops essentially become increasingly difficult bossfights against the merchant, with assorted magical items as loot. The merchant itself will become increasingly powerful, warped and repulsive. The masks come into play here.
