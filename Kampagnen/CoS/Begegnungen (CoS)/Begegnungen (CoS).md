###### *Rahadin.*
Kommt, um sich im Namen seines Herren und Bruders zu entschuldigen. Wo Elfen ihre Persönlichkeiten im Alter festigen, sind Menschen für so viele Jahrhunderte nicht gemacht. Strahd hat vergessen/vergraben, wer er wirklich ist. Rahadin glaubt an die Nachfolger-Idee, aber nur Savas (als Adliger) wäre potenziell würdig.
Außerdem will er Rowan erzählen, dass er dessen Vater zu Tode gefoltert hat. 

###### *Framed.*
*Replaces the Barovian Scouts encounter, if it's been encountered at least once before.*
There is a strange rhythm to the birds' cries around you. Echoing low in the mists. Shuffling leaves, a distant dog and a short bark. Passive Wisdom (Nature or Perception, DC 17 or the Scout Captain's stealth check) will betray the "birds" as whistled signals, or reveal the silhouettes stalking you just off the path.
A [[Scout-Captain|Scout Captain]] leading 1d4+1 Barovian [[Scout|Scout]]s and 1d6 [[01-Rules/Statblocks/Beasts/Mastiff|Mastiff]]s, originating from the closes active settlement.
They carry drawings of the party, which accuse them of some heinous, unforgivable crime. The crime is real, but the party did not perpetrate it. Strahd tricked some brave Barovians  into "enacting justice." It's part of his game. Can he corrupt the party into slaughtering some ordinary people? Undermine the relationship between his subjects and their want-to-be "saviors"? 
(Either way, the plot has not spread far. The search party assembled hastily and in the early hours, and took the "wanted" posters with them. Their home settlement isn't fully aware why the hunters left, and they don't consider the player characters wanted fugitives.)
The search party is prepared for a fight, but will reveal themselves and offer a chance to surrender. With their crossbows at the ready, the party still suffers disadvantage on initiative unless they spotted the search party. 
The search party prefers to capture the party alive and bring them to justice. They're wary of the foreigners and their power, especially if the party already has a reputation for using magic. If obviously outmatched, they won't hesitate to retreat from combat. Each scout gets one death save.
*To make them slightly more powerful*, feel free to grant the Scouts the mastery properties of their weapons ([[01-Rules/Weapon Properties/Masteries/Slow|Slow]] for [[Light-Crossbow]]s and [[Vex]] for Shortswords and Handaxes.)
***Treasure.*** [[Light-Crossbow]]s, [[Shortsword]]s, or [[Handaxe]]s. 1d10ep on each scout. [[Scout-Captain|Scout Captain]] carries a [[herbalism-kit]] and 1d4 [[Potions of Healing]] (will use them to save her companions). 
The Mastiffs are loyal to their masters and stubbornly fight to the death. If they can still be pacified or restrained, it takes at least 2h and Animal Handling (DC20) to "adopt" them. [[Speak-with-Animals]] grants advantage on the check.

- *If the scouts get the drop on the party* and the party decides to fight back, they are surrounded, and half of the scouts are hidden 20 ft. up in tree canopies.

- *If the party flees the scouts,* determine a random PC to roll a DC 16 (Scout Captain's passive survival) Perception check. *Failure:* They fail to spot and are caught by a [[hunting-trap]].

- *If the party surrenders to the scouts*, their weapons, armor and obvious arcane/religious implements are taken and they are bound with [[rope]]. Strahd, suspecting that the PCs would prove their innocence in trial and sensing an opportunity to eliminate some of Barovia's best hunters, sends his wolves. If the party has not killed any scouts and they are still bound after the first round of combat, the scouts will try to to cut them loose, or toss them a knife to free themselves. 

- *If the party negotiates their way out of combat* (DC20), the scouts will ask them to accompany them back to the closest settlement to clear things up. If they accept, the Wolf ambush occurs on the way back.

- *The Wolves* ([[wolf|Wolf]]) arrive as a pack of 3d6 and 1d6 [[Dire-Wolf|Dire Wolves]]. Their objective is to kill and abduct the [[Scout-Captain|Scout Captain]]. If clearly outmatched and without their captain, remaining scouts will despair and flee. If all [[Dire-Wolf|Dire Wolves]] are slain, the pack retreats. A creature who is 20+ ft. up in a tree is safe from wolf attacks, but any [[Dire-Wolf]] can use an action to shake the tree, imposing a DC 13 strength save on creatures in the tree to avoid falling. (Once per tree.)
***In the long run...*** 
- *if the Scout Captain is lost to the Wolves*, Barovia's hunters are thoroughly demoralized. Whatever tenuous courage they had is shattered by the sudden feasibility of coordinated, daytime attacks. From now on, when the characters are travelling Barovia, a random encounter occurs on a 17-20.
- *if the Scout Captain or her body are brought back to Castle Ravenloft*, the *Barovian Scouts* and *Hunting Trap* encounters are replaced by an ambush of 1d4+1 [[Vampire-Spawn|Vampire Spawn]] armed with Light Crossbows. One of them has the ***Uncanny Dodge*** reaction and the ***Aim*** bonus action, as well as proficiency with Dex and Int saves and the maximum hp (132). Treat her CR as increased by 1.



###### *Hero's Rest.*
*Encountered with the parade of souls, if the party has seen it before. Substitute for the will 'o wisp encounter.*
The souls make their way to Castle Ravenloft with blind determination. But something catches your eye. A single ghostly form suddenly strays from the path. It moves with a strange nervousness, as if trying to sneak off. 
As it disappears into the trees, you notice more movement. One by one, souls step into the woods. 
The parade doesn't slow, as if unaware it's ranks are thinner now.
If you track the missing specters, you find yourself on an overgrown path. The heads of scattered cobblestones still stick out, split by roots and mud.
After only moments, you reach a clearing. It hosts the husk of a building, crumbling under burned rubble. A face-down wooden sign out front says "Hero's Rest" in rotten letters. 
The ghosts step through the empty doorway, fading through the rubble. There's a sudden whiff of food and drink, an echo of clinking glasses, then a muffled scream. Then, the first ghost steps out the back of a building, a gashing cut over their throat, and lumbers into the remains of a barn. 

The entire clearing is desecrated ground.

When the characters wait till the last ghost disappears inside the barn, a [[Gallows Speaker.png]] emerges. It attacks, but desperately tries to relay the story of what happened here.

This place was an inn, operated by the same cult who resided in Barovia's Death House. Seeking to appease Strahd, they lured adventurers with food and drink, cut their throats in their sleep and feed them to the pigs. 

Players who investigate the main house may find letters from the inhabitants of Death House, and unsent letters flattering Strahd. 

Players who investigate the pigpen find adventurers' remains, including some potentially useful loot. They also surprise a litter of 2d6 overgrown adolescent pigs ([[Boar|Boar]]). On emerging, they find themselves surrounded by 1d6 monstrous pigs ([[Giant-Boar]]). They promptly drop the remains of an unfortunate barovian scout to attack the intruders.
The litter may attempt to protect their parents, but will flee if the adults are killed.

If the party neither kills nor tames the pigs, they keep killing scouts and hunters. The next time the party would encounter at least one [[wolf|Wolf]]/[[Dire-Wolf]] through a random encounter, each is replaced by a flesh-eating pig of equal size.

***From now on...*** the clearing is no longer desecrated and no further villagers go missing. However, the ghosts are not yet free. They are drawn back into the parade, sustaining the heart of sorrow for an additional 50 HP.

###### *Sunburn.*
*Only encountered if the party has destroyed a Vampire Spawn using sunlight. May replace any Vampire Spawn encounter.*
Something's wrong in the mists. There's an iron taste to it. 
The party finds an impossible trail of blood. It's glisteningly fresh, made up of tiny drops. There are no other tracks. If they follow the trail, it leads them into a trap. 
At any point, if a character looks up:
The mists are all around you. Above you. Thick. Above you. Coagulating. Dripping bright red. Turning bright red.
If no one looks up, sooner or later, a character is hit in the face by a drop of blood and the mist attacks, with disadvantage on the initiative roll for the party.
A [[Crimson Mist.jpg]] attacks. It's the blood of a Vampire Spawn slain by the party, and it's here to take revenge. It will take a humanoid shape and make itself known to the party. 
It uses the improved mental stats outlined in the statblock, and has the maximum hp (96).
***From now on...*** whenever the party kills a Vampire Spawn using sunlight, roll a d20. On 18-20, a [[Crimson Mist.jpg]] forms, acting on the slain spawn's initiative. 

###### *Bloodletting.*
*Once the party encountered 1d8 Swarms of Bats (nr. 9 on the nighttime Barovia random encounter table): Replace it with the following encounter.*
Muffled moans in the mists ahead. The path is lined by 1d4+1 [[Vampire Spawn]], impaled on sturdy wooden pikes. Each is placed 15 ft. away from the others, with the spawn hanging 10 ft. above ground. 
The Spawn are [[Restrained]] (DC 15 to free, 25 to escape), gagged and [[Blinded]] (their eyes cut out). Until interacted with, they are only half-conscious, in a blood-drunken daze. 
Crude signs suggest that these are punished murderers or servants of Strahd, brought low by brave Barovians and left to rot. 
In reality, they are Vampire Spawn placed here by Strahd, to punish them or simply out of boredom. Strahd is running an experiment to create [[Strigoi.png]] under his control, using the blood of his subservient spawn.
The Vampire Spawn are:
1. Innocent Barovians. Memories fuzzy. Begging for help. Apparently unaware of their transformation. Met the party when they were alive.
2. Previously encountered Vampire Spawn. Placed here for failing to kill the party. Hope to redeem themselves.
3. Discarded Brides. In denial. Claim Strahd is coming to free them.
4. Doru.
5. Fallen Adventurers. Will try to bargain and manipulate for freedom. Claim to 1) be rebels fighting Strahd, 2) share their (fictitious) stash of magical equipment, 3) know a cure for vampirism, 4) know a way out of Barovia.
6. Discarded Vistani. Accidentally irritated Strahd. He likes to present them like this to stoke conflict between Barovians and Vistani. Still barely alive, but pumped full of vampire blood. Will die and turn within 1d4 hours. Spiteful, willing to divulge any information (truthful or not) to be freed or hurt Strahd.
Removing their gags will reveal blood-soaked fangs. They are freshly fed. For the purposes of staking them, they qualify as [[Incapacitated]]. If prodded, they may:
Roll Initiative when:
- A vampire spawn is hurt. They now attempt to free themselves.
- ~5 minutes have passed.
- Significant noise is made.
A freed Vampire Spawn attempts to drain blood from the characters or flee immediately. The first time a Vampire Spawn restores hp, they regenerate their eyes and are no longer blinded.
Surviving Vampire Spawn return to Castle Ravenloft to apologize to Strahd. Strahd rewards them by turning them into [[Nosferatu.jpg]] and sending them back out to kill the party.
When initiative is rolled, 1 [[Swarm-of-Stirges|Swarm of Stirges]] appear and descend on each Vampire Spawn. On their turns, they attempt to attach (using their Proboscis) and drain the Spawns' blood. When a Stirge Swarm is reduced to 0 hp by a non-Area-of-Effect source, 1d4 surviving stirges are left in it's space.
A Stirge or Stirge Swarm who successfully deals any necrotic damage to a Vampire Spawn immediately grows and transforms into a [[Strigoi.png]]. A Stirge Swarm transformed this way leaves behind 1d4+2 [[Stirge]]s under the Strigoi's control.
The Strigoi will spend 1 turn counterattacking if hurt, but flee on their next turn, leaving the Stirges behind to cover their retreat. They are sated and distracted by their newfound intelligence.
***From now on...***  if at least one Strigoi is hatched during this encounter:
Strahd has gained control over a rapidly growing host of Stirges and an easy method of creating more Strigoi. From now on while in Barovia, whenever the party would encounter one or more:
- [[Swarm-of-Bats]], Strahd may have them encounter an equal number of [[Swarm-of-Stirges|Swarm of Stirges]] instead. 
- Vampire Spawn, Strahd may have them encounter 1d8+1 [[Strigoi.png]] instead.
- [[Zombie|Zombie]]s, Strahd may replace them with [[Boneless.png]], with clearly visible proboscis wounds

###### *The Lonely.*
*Unique. Replaces 20 (Strahd Zombies) on the Village of Barovia house encounter table.*
Bei Betreten des Hauses steht der Gruppe der [[TheHungry_TheLonely.png|Lonely]] gegenüber. Er greift sofort an, um die Gruppe seiner bizarren Choreo hinzuzufügen.
***Omen.*** Wer [[Divine Sense]] verwendet, spürt mehrere Untote. Allerdings ist etwas seltsam: Einige Zombies streifen rastlos durch ihre Häuser, als würden sie alten Gewohnheiten folgen. Selbst die trägsten bewegen sich ein wenig. Diese Gruppe jedoch ist vollständig bewegungslos.
**DC 10 Perception.** Verwesungsgeruch, sowie in anderen Häusern mit Untoten.
**DC 20 Perception.** Du hörst eine leise Stimme. Es klingt wie das unerlässliche und weinerliche Brabbeln eines Kleinkindes.
2w4 [[Fluch des Strahd.pdf#page=242|Strahdzombies]] sind bewegungsunfähig im Haus verteilt, mit Drähten befestigt. Sie wurden wie Puppen im Puppenhaus drapiert und verkleidet. Jede Attacke im Haus, die Hiebschaden verursacht und kein Treffer war, zerschneidet einen Faden und befreit einen Strahdzombie. Die Zombies sind feindlich gegenüber allen nicht-untoten Kreaturen, inkl. dem Lonely. Sollte ein Zombie zu Schaden kommen, wird der Lonely auf jämmerliche Art und Weise seinen Namen rufen (er hat alle Zombies benannt), und seinen nächsten Angriff gegen den Verursacher richten. Er greift die Zombies nicht an, und sie sind immun gegen seine **Psychic Leech** Aura.

Sollten die Charaktere das Haus verlassen, folgt der Lonely *nur*, wenn die folgenden Bedingungen befriedigt sind:
1. Es ist Nacht und er ist nicht bloodied.
**ODER**:
2. Die Gruppe hat einen oder mehrere seiner Zombies vernichtet. In diesem Fall will er Rache nehmen und kämpft bis zum Tod.

Unter folgenden Umständen wird er das Haus *nicht* verlassen:
1. Mind. 1 seiner Zombies sind befreit und im Haus.
2. Mind. 1 Spielercharakter ist noch im Haus (tot oder lebendig.)

Wenn die Gruppe den Lonely im Haus zurücklässt, können sie hören, wie er unter gebrabbeltem Schluchzen seine "Familie" beruhigt. Währenddessen fällt diese über ihn her (falls alle Zombies noch gefesselt sind, beschreibe das peitschende Geräusch, als Fäden unter der Spannung reißen). Reißendes Fleisch und knachende Knochen werden das Jammern des Lonely früher oder später ersticken, der sich dieser letzten Umarmung wehrlos hingibt.

###### *Infestation.*
*Unique. Replaces 8 (Rat Swarms) on the Village of Barovia House encounters.*
Im Haus befindet sich ein [[Infestation Swarm]] im Körper eines Kindes (Commoner), sowie 2 [[Swarm of Maggots, Scarabs.jpg|Maggot Swarms]] und 1w4 Swarm of Insects. 
Von außen ein verlassenes, schmutziges Haus, ähnlich wie alle Rattenhäuser. Divine Sense registriert keine Untoten. Sollte die Gruppe Anklopfen, können sie Schritte von innen hören. Sie nähern sich der Tür, ohne sie zu öffnen. (*Der Schwarm hat nicht den erforderlichen Intellekt, um einen Türgriff zu benutzen. Möglicherweise hört man jedoch ein Kratzen oder Klopfen, beim vergeblichen Versuch.*)
Die Tür ist nicht abgeschlossen. Drinnen wartet *Nianka,* eine barovische Frau Anfang 20. Sie ist noch am Leben, aber sie dient dem Schwarm als Wirtin und kann nicht sprechen. 
Diese junge Frau ist dem Tode nahe. Was auch immer die Krankheit, an der sie leidet, sie wirkt dehydriert, abgemagert bis auf die Knochen. 
**DC 15 Insight.** Wenn in dieser kümmerlichen Gestalt noch ein Mensch steckt, dann in ihren Augen. Unter hängenden Augenlidern sind hektische, gerötete Pupillen, die euren Blick mit einer Mischung aus Panik und Warnung suchen.
**DC 15 Medicine.** Sie zeigt Symptome von Entbehrung und womöglich einer Vergiftung, aber keiner offensichtlichen Seuche o.ä. Doch da ist noch etwas: Seltsame Muskelzuckungen. Bei genauerer Betrachtung wirkt es, als ob sich unter ihrer Haut etwas bewegt.
Die obenstehenden Informationen können auch mit einer DC 20 Perception Probe herausgefunden werden.
Bei einem Misserfolg, oder wenn die Gruppe den Rest des Hauses erkundet o.ä.
Nianka's Eltern betreten die Stube. Sie sind offensichtlich tot, animiert von Madenschwärmen. Sie von gewöhnlichen Zombies zu unterscheiden erfordert eine Fähigkeitsprobe (Perception 25/Medicine 20).
Sobald sie angreifen oder Schaden nehmen, zerbrechen die weichen Leichen, und die Maden brechen in ihrer normalen Form hervor und greifen an.
Die Insektenschwärme brechen aus den Wänden, sobald der Kampf beginnt. Der Infestation Swarm verlässt Nianka, wenn Nianka getötet wird (sie hat 1TP), oder wenn er nach der 1. Kampfrunde noch in Nianka ist.

###### *Schattenspiel.*
Ein abgelegenes Anwesen, eine vergessene Villa, ein verlassener Teil von Schloss Ravenloft, eine Ruine. 1w4 [[Darkling-mpmm|Darkling]] sind hier versteckt, und 3w4 Schatten imitieren das Leben ihrer toten Besitzer. Eindringlinge, die grelles Licht bringen, werden angegriffen. Die Darklings sind fähig und gewillt zur Kommunikation, aber nur im Dunkeln, und nur auf Sylvan. 

###### *Bitte, hör auf zu Weinen.*
*Ein weinendes Kind wird von den Eltern erst mit Gleichgültigkeit, dann mit Züchtigung zum Schweigen gebracht. Auf Intervention oder Nachfrage erklären sie, dass Kinder die Weinen oder Lachen vom dunklen Fürsten gestohlen werden (Jene mit starken Gefühlsausdrücken haben eine Seele, und kommen damit als Beute für Strahd in Frage)*

###### *Blinker*
Ein verletzter [[Blink-Dog]] erscheint aus dem Nichts. Er vertraut [[Kellan]] instinktiv und kann ein Vertrauter werden. Seine Wunden sind vergiftet: Sein Angreifer war der [[Wickerutt.jpg]]. Es gibt noch eine Möglichkeit, davonzukommen.
