##### 1. Fight
*A group of Scouts from the Village of Barovia is carefully making their way to Castle Ravenloft. They're just ahead of the main force, scouting for threats.*
Doru, *Son of* Donawitsch *is with them, eager to challenge the Devil Strahd.*
Round 1: 1d6 [[Scout]]s and Doru are ambushed by 3d6 [[wolf|Wolves]] at the [[River Ivlis Crossroads (Day)]].
End of Round (Initiative count 1), 1d4 [[Swarm of Ravens]] swoop in to help the Scouts and drive off the Wolves.

Before Initiative:
If the Wolves succeed on a collective Dexterity (Stealth) check against the Scouts passive Perception, the Scouts are surprised and have disadvantage on the initiative roll. 
(Use Group Initiative.)

Setup:
The Wolves have encircled the Scouts. 1d6 of them may start within 40 ft. or less of an enemy, the rest is further away.
The Scouts and Doru were moving as a group, sticking to cover but with an eye on the road.
The Ravens are hiding in trees, ignored by the combatants until they descend.

*The self-proclaimed Envoy of the Morninglord has fallen to Strahd von Zarovich. The Revolution is scattered. Many, including Doru, are dead or captured.

*As the bridge peels from the darkness, so do human shapes. Their lurching, twitchy movements quickly disperses any hope to meet fellow survivors.*
3d6 [[Zombie]]s are scattered between the Commoners and the bridge. 2d6 of them are [[Prone]] when combat starts.

On Initiative Count 1, a hooded figure ([[Bandit Captain]]) emerges from a hiding spot to assist the Commoners.

On Initiative Count 20, if a commoner has crossed the bridge, 1d8 [[Swarm of Bats]] descend from the night sky and attack the commoners.
