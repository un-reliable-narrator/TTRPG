However, it presents problems. Or rather, the [[Kampagnen/CoS/Statblocks (CoS)/Wereraven.png]] statblock does. It's regeneration trait would mean player characters gain free, passive regeneration AND could only die if hit by silvered/magical damage.
Do we interpret the wording such that the Wereraven *always* (i.e. a sufficient condition) dies when it doesn't regenerate at the start of it's turn (which would effectively remove PC's death saves)? 
If so, it would still be worth it for the fly speed and the regeneration, and the ability to slay endless hordes of nonmagical enemies. 
If we are lenient, and assume that not regenerating and having 0hp at start of turn is not a *sufficient*, but a *necessary* condition, *in addition* to the condition for a PC to have 3 failed death saves, the trait becomes even more powerful. 

In order to kill a Wereraven PC, an enemy must reduce them to 0 hit points and deal magical damage to them on the consecutive turn(s), until they accrue 3 failed death saves. A PC, even when struck down by magical damage, CAN NOT DIE without taking further (magical) damage while unconscious. In fact, there is a 79.75% chance that killing a PC requires hitting them TWICE or more. 

Assuming a PC was reduced to 0 hp by magical damage, their next rounds may look like this:
1) Death Save (45% of failure)
2) If magical damage was taken: Death Save. With one failure from the damage, you could now be dead, IF you rolled 2 consecutive failures. The chance of this is 20.25%. 

#### Implications, Thoughts, Solutions
---
I like weird rules. PC's are meant to become powerful. But to accommodate this power, the antagonists will need to scale up significantly. In other words: Giving Wereraven Lycanthropy to my group should be done after a significant defeat, in a moment of severe desperation.
Possible Conditions:
- The Winery is destroyed, and the Keepers of the Feather are desperate
- The Party went to challenge Strahd at Castle Ravenloft, and lost
- The Party was robbed of another significant source of their power (spellbooks, magic items, levels, ability scores, class features)
- The party was weakened in some significant way (cursed, mutilated)
- A significant task failed/asset was lost (e.g. allies killed, treasures lost, skull/beacon of Argynvost destroyed)
- One of the settlements of Barovia is destroyed
- Strahd (and his forces) have powered up significantly, through:
	- Determined recruitment
	- Imports from the outside world
	- Strahd absorbing some significant power
	- The Dark Powers
	- Finally getting serious
	- Blessings of Mother Night
