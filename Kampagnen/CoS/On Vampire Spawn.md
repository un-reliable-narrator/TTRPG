## Spielercharaktere als Vampire
---
*Sometimes, a slain player may return as a Vampire Spawn.*

#### Existing Features
---
A Vampire Spawn usually does not lose any of their class features. 

***Paladins*** may return with their oath inverted into a twisted, evil version, determined by Strahd. 
- Any radiant damage they would deal turns into necrotic damage.
- Any light they produce becomes ghostly faded moonlight.
Alternatively, they may fight a losing struggle against the hunger and Strahd's commands to uphold their oath.
- Any radiant damage they deal is also inflicted upon themselves. 

***Clerics*** have their divine connection severed. The power they draw on now is granted by the Dark Powers.
- Any radiant damage they would deal turns into necrotic damage.
- Any light they produce becomes ghostly faded moonlight.

The same pattern holds true for any other class feature or ability that draws on divine power and deals radiant damage.



![[vampirespawn.png]]

***Ability Scores.*** A Strength, Dexterity or Constitution score lower than 16 is increased to 16. ~~(If 16 or higher: +1.)~~

***Saves.*** Gain proficiency in Dexterity saves.

***Necrotic Resistance.*** Gain resistance to necrotic damage.

***Hunter's Instincts.*** Become proficient in Perception and Stealth.

***Senses.*** Gain 60 ft. Darkvision.

~~***Hit Dice.*** If lower than a d8: All HD become d8. If fewer than 12: Increase to 12.~~ (Vampire Spawn NPCs usually have 90+ health.)

~~***Proficiency Bonus.*** If lower than 3: Becomes 3.~~

***Creature Type.*** You are undead.

#### Old Blood
---
Vampire Spawn sired by Strahd von Zarovich may gain Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage and the ***Regeneration*** trait.

#### Traits
---
*A former player character, when turned into a Vampire Spawn, gains the following traits (if they don't have them already.)*
***Spider Climb.***
***Vampire Weakness.*** 

***Devil's Thrall.*** You are not a full vampire. Your only way to ascend is to drink the blood of your Sire, freely offered or obtained through murder. If your Sire issues you a command, you are compelled to obey.
You are under the effect of a permanent [[Dominate-Person]] spell originating from your Sire (no concentration required). You may repeat the save against the effect when your Sire's orders would force you to harm an ally.
The spell is automatically cast on you again whenever you finish a rest. 
Additionally, you have disadvantage on all saves against effects originating from your Sire that would charm or command you (including [[Dominate-Person]]).
*(When you lose this trait, e.g. by ascending from Vampire Spawn to a True Vampire, the spell ends immediately.)*
~~To resist a command, make a Wisdom Save (DC 18, or 27 if your Sire is Strahd Von Zarovich.)~~ 

***Eternal Thirst.*** 
You cannot benefit from a short or long rest. Instead gain all the benefits and effects of a long rest by spending 2h drinking the blood of a freshly killed humanoid (small or larger). 
You can also gain the benefits of a short rest, by feeding on a Humanoid or Beast for at least 30 minutes. Your victim takes necrotic damage equal to the hit points you healed.
For each day you go without drinking blood, gain 1 exhaustion.
While you are exhausted, you have disadvantage on saves to resist the urge to feed.
(You do not require food or sleep. Your body cannot process food.) 
If the fresh, warm blood of an intelligent creature is drawn within 30 ft: Make a Wisdom save (DC 10). You may automatically succeed if you are at full hp and not exhausted.
*Failure:* You must feed. Your spend your next turn attempting to approach and feed on a humanoid of your choice within reach. You may employ social means like coercion, but you must resort to violence if not successful.
*Success*: You resist. For 1h, you may ignore all saves against hunger.
