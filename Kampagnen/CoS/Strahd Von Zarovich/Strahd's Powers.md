I want the party to both experience Strahd's "RAW" statblock, and be able to somewhat hold their ground in occasional confrontations, as well as power him up for a final battle.
Strahd should have a scheme to gain a fundamental power-up, which the party can frustrate and delay, and possibly weaken or alter.

Maybe he can gain certain powers by draining people? Including PC's? At least temporarily absorb them.

###### [[Animate-Objects]]
In Castle Ravenloft, while not in combat, Strahd can cast [[Animate-Objects]] with these modifications:
- Duration becomes permanent.
- Concentration (or giving commands) is not required. 
- Brooms and Swords become [[Animated-Broom|Animated Broom]]s and [[Animated Flying Sword]]s
- Armors become [[Animated Armor]]s
- Rugs become [[Animated Rug of Smothering]]
*Effectively, he can add 5 Swords/Brooms/Armors or half that in Rugs to the Castle's defenses every day.*

##### Actions
---
***Multiattack (Vampire Form Only).*** Strahd makes two Unarmed Strikes and uses his bite attack.

***Unarmed Strike (Vampire Form Only).*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) Bludgeoning damage plus 14 (4d6) Necrotic damage. ==If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the [[Grappled]] condition (escape DC 17) from one of two hands.==

==***Bite  (Bat or Vampire Form Only).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 17, one creature within 5 feet that is willing or that has the [[Grappled]], [[Incapacitated]], or [[Restrained]] condition. *Failure:* 7 (1d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) Necrotic damage. The target's Hit Point maximum decreases by an amount equal to the Necrotic damage taken, and the vampire regains Hit Points equal to that amount. A Humanoid reduced to 0 Hit Points by this damage and then buried rises the following sunset as a **[[Vampire Spawn]]** under the vampire's control.==

##### Bonus Actions
---
***Charm (Recharge 5-6).*** The vampire casts *[[Charm-Person]]*, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17), and the duration is 24 hours. The [[Charmed]] target is a willing recipient of the vampire's Bite, the damage of which doesn't end the spell. When the spell ends, the target is unaware it was Charmed by the vampire.
==(Replaces Charm Action)==

***Shapechanger.*** ==(Moved from Action to Bonus Action)==

##### Legendary Actions
---
*Legendary Action Uses: 3 ==(4 in Lair)==. Immediately after another creature's turn, the vampire can expend a use to take one of the following actions. The vampire regains all expended uses at the start of each of its turns.*
***Deathless Strike.*** Strahd moves up to half has Speed, and it makes one Unarmed Strike attack.
***Beguile.*** The vampire casts *[[Command]]*, requiring no spell components and using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 17). The vampire can't take this action again until the start of its next turn.
