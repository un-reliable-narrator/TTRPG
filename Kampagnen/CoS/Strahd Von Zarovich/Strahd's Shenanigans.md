Strahd wants to *break* the party, and while he has the trappings and habits of a noble, there's no depravity or behavior he wouldn't indulge in. Over the centuries, he has seized caring about his image. 
His schemes are cruel, nonsensical and, at times, childish. The first guiding principle is ***Humor***, objectives are secondary to the punchline.
Obviously, he has "serious" goals, too, but they take a backseat against short-term fun sometimes.
