### Reactions

***Chronal Shift. (2/day)*** *Trigger*: You or a creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw. *Response:* You force the creature to reroll. You make this decision after you see whether the roll succeeds or fails. The target must use the result of the second roll.

***Convergent Future.*** *Trigger:* You or a creature you can see within 60 feet of you makes an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw. *Response:* Ignore the die roll and decide whether the number rolled is the minimum needed to succeed or one less than that number (your choice).
When you use this feature, you gain one level of exhaustion. Only by finishing a long rest can you remove a level of exhaustion gained in this way.
