Spellcasting.

Leo is a 9th-level spellcaster. The leopold hamperding's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks). The leopold hamperding has the following sorcerer spells prepared:

Cantrip (at will): minor illusion, mage hand
1st level (4 slots): charm person, detect magic, disguise self, expeditious retreat, fog cloud
2nd level (3 slots): hold person, blindness/deafness, alter self, detect thoughts, spider climb
3rd level (3 slots): dispel magic, counterspell, stinking cloud, haste
4th level (3 slots): greater invisibility, confusion
5th level (1 slots): dominate person, telekinesis, teleportation circle

Actions

Multiattack.

Leo makes two dagger attacks.

Dagger.

Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage

Reactions

It's not what it looks like, I swear.

If a humanoid enemy targets Leo with a melee attack, he may force the attacker to make an Int save (DC 16). On a failure, the attacker's turn ends immediately. On a success, the attacker becomes immune to this ability.

Legendary Actions

Leo can take legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Leo regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

Flee.

If Leo is adjacent to an enemy, he may move up to his movement speed without triggering opportunity attacks.

Panic.

Leo casts a spell, targeting himself (if the spell targets a creature) or the space he is in (if the spell targets an area).

Description

Easy smiles and easy lies. An enigmatic spellcasting trickster with erratic tactics and unkown motives. Acted as Arthur's parttime tutor and 'sponsor'.
