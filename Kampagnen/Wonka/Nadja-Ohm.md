*Celestial Empress and Tyrant of this World.*

## NADJA OHM
*Medium, humanoid, Sorcerer 20 (Lunar Magic)*

**AC** 19 ([[Robe-of-the-Archmagi|Robe of the Archmagi]])
**HP** 193 (20d6 + 120) ([[01-Rules/Feats/Origin Feats/Tough|Tough]]) **Temporary HP** 5 (Bolstering Treat)
**Speed** 30 ft.

**Str** 16 **Dex** 18 + 4 **Con** 18
**Int** 14 **Wis** 16 **Cha** 24

**Skills** Intimidation +13
**Senses** Passive Perception 20
**Saves** Wis +9, Con +10, Cha +13
**Resistances** Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Radiant
**Gear** [[Robe-of-the-Archmagi|Robe of the Archmagi]], [[Wand-of-the-War-Mage|Wand of the War Mage +3]], [[ioun-Stone|Ioun Stone of Absorption]], [[Potion-of-Supreme-healing|Potion of Supreme Healing]] (x3), [[Scroll of the Comet]]. [[Chef|Bolstering Treats]] (x5)
**Level** 20 (PB +6)

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** (Robe of the Archmagi) Advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.

**Lunar Phases.** NADJA always benefits from one of these effects:
- **Full Moon:** You shed bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet. In addition, you and creatures of your choice have advantage on saving throws while within the bright light you shed.
- **New Moon:** You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. In addition, while you are in dim light or darkness, attack rolls have disadvantage against you.
- **Crescent Moon:** You have resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.

**Magically Enhanced.** NADJA has cast [[Mirror-Image]] and [[Death-Ward]] on herself. (Using her handmaidens to extend the duration by 24 hours.)

**Metamagic.** NADJA uses her [[list-optfeaturetype-mm|Metamagic]] in the following ways:
- **[[empowered-spell|Empowered Spell]] (1 SP)**: For any damaging spell, NADJA may reroll up to  6 damage dice (once each). *Always used.*
- **[[heightened-spell|Heightened Spell]] (2 SP)**: For spells that force a single creature to make a save, NADJA imposes disadvantage on that save. *Used for Hold Person.*
- **[[quickened-spell|Quickened Spell]] (2 SP):** Rarely, to dodge, dash, or attack with a cantrip in addition to casting a spell.
- **[[subtle-spell|Subtle Spell]] (1 SP:)** For dramatic effect, or when cornered in specific ways.

**Handmaidens**. NADJA has a retinue of handmaidens ([[Apprentice-Wizard-mpmm|Apprentice Wizard]]), youths with arcane promise drafted from subjugated worlds who assist her spellcasting. While alive and within 30 ft. of her, they let her use the following [[Circle Magic]] options:
- **[[Circle Magic#Augment|Augment]]**: Cast from great distance.
- **Expand**: Obliterate armies.
- **Distribute**: Enables NADJA to concentrate on an almost unlimited number of spells.
- **Safeguard**: Creates a number of 5 ft. cubes that are excepted from the area of effect of a spell. Used to protect the handmaidens.
- **[[Circle Magic#Prolong|Prolong]]:** Used to extend her buff spells.

### Actions

***Spellcasting.*** NADJA casts one of the following spells, using an available spell slot of sufficient level (spell save DC 23):
- **2nd-level:** [[Hold-Person]] *(with [[heightened-spell|Heightened Spell]])*
- **3rd-level:** [[Dispel-Magic|Dispel Magic]], [[Fireball]]
- **5th-level:** [[Cone-of-Cold]], [[Wall-of-Stone]]
- **6th-level:** [[Mass-Suggestion]], [[Chain-Lightning]]
- **7th-level:** [[Fire-Storm]], [[Reverse-Gravity]]
- **8th-level:** [[Sunburst]]

***Lunar Spellcasting.*** Depending on her lunar phase, NADJA casts one of the following spells (spell save DC 23):
- **Full Moon (1/day each):** [[Faerie-Fire]], [[Moonbeam]], [[Death-Ward]], [[Freedom-of-Movement]], [[Mass-Cure-Wounds]]
- **New Moon (1/day each:**) [[Dissonant-Whispers]], [[01-Rules/Spells/Core/Level 2/Darkness|Darkness]], [[Bestow-Curse]], [[Evard's-Black-Tentacles]], [[Mislead]]
- **Crescent Moon (1/day each:)** [[Blindness-Deafness]]

***Sorcerous Burst.*** *Ranged spell attack:* +18, range 120 ft. *Hit:* 4d8 Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, Psychic or Thunder damage. For each 8 on a d8 included in the damage roll, add 1d8 of damage.

***Sacred Flame.*** Dexterity Save, two targets you can see within 60 ft. *Failure:* 4d8 radiant damage.

### Bonus Actions

***Spellcasting.*** NADJA casts one of the following spells, using [[quickened-spell|Quickened Spell]] and an available spell slot of sufficient level (spell save DC 23):
- **3rd-level:** [[Dispel-Magic]], [[Haste]]
- **4th-level:** [[Banishment]], [[Dimension-Door]]

***Full Moon (1/day, or 5 SP)*** NADJA's lunar phase becomes Full Moon if it isn't already. She may radiate intense moonlight for a moment. Each creature of her choice within 30 feet of her must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against her spell save DC or be blinded until the end of its next turn. In addition, one creature of her choice in that area regains 3d8 hit points.

***New Moon (1/day, or 5 SP)*** You momentarily emanate an oppressive gloom. Each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC or take 3d10 necrotic damage and have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn. In addition, you become invisible until the end of your next turn, or until you make an attack or cast a spell.

***Crescent Moon (1/day, or 5 SP)*** You slip through light and darkness. You can magically teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within 60 feet of yourself. In addition, you also gain resistance to all damage until the start of your next turn.

### Reactions

***[[Ioun-Stone-Absorption|Ioun Stone, Absorption]]***. Cancel a spell of level 4 or lower cast by a creature you can see. A canceled spell has no effect, and any resources used to cast it are wasted. Once the stone has canceled 20 levels of spells, it burns out, turns dull gray, and loses its magic.

***Shield***. NADJA casts [[01-Rules/Spells/Core/Level 1/Shield|Shield]], using an appropriate spell slot.

***Energy Redirection.*** When NADJA takes fire or lightning damage, she can take a [Reaction](Reaction.md) to direct damage of the same type toward another creature she can see within 60 feet of herself that isn't behind [Total Cover](Total Cover). If she does, that creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 18) or take `dice: 2d12 + 4`damage.
