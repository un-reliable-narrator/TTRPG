# ORO
*Coiling Oracle, Lord of Lies and Tyrant of this World.*

**Ever-Married.** ORO remarries every week. The suitor must be an adult humanoid made willing to be bonded. (E.g. by the promise of after-death rewards.) Though some suitors "survive", the marriage always concludes with their death after 6/7 days. (In order to satisfy [[Ceremony]]'s requirement to become widowed.) 
ORO could have re-married, killed and revivified the same person to the same outcome. This never occurred to them, and instead they replace their suitors weekly.
These days, the suitors are almost exclusively Legacy Warforged (with the humanoid typing) and [[Reborn]]. This is because ORO prefers to keep them within 30 ft. by placing them in a bag of holding, which they then place inside of their own body.
All of it is done for +2 AC.

### Traits

**Magic Resistance.** (Yuan-ti)

**Eldritch Invocations.** ORO benefits from nine Eldritch Invocations:
- **Gift of the Protectors (1/day)**. When ORO is reduced to 0 [Hit Points](app://obsidian.md/hit-points.md) but not killed outright, ORO magically drops to 1 [Hit Point](app://obsidian.md/hit-points.md) instead. Requires ORO's book of shadow to be intact.
- **Eldritch Mind**. ORO has advantage on concentration saves.
- **[[fiendish-vigor|Fiendish Vigor]]**, [[Agonizing-Blast]], [[repelling-blast|Repelling Blast]], [[eldritch-spear|Eldritch Spear]], [[pact-of-the-tome|Pact of the Tome]], [[gaze-of-two-minds|Gaze of Two Minds]], [[lessons-of-the-first-ones|Lessons of the First Ones]] (included in the statblock). 

**Thought Shield.** Whenever a creature deals Psychic damage to ORO, that creature takes the same amount of damage ORO takes.

**Strength of the Grave.** If you would drop to 0 Hit Points and not die outright, you can make a Charisma saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken). On a successful save, your Hit Points instead change to a number equal to your Charisma modifier plus your Sorcerer level. After you succeed on this save, you can’t use this benefit again until you finish a Long Rest.

**Psychic Spells.** When you cast a Warlock spell that deals damage, you can change its damage type to Psychic. In addition, when you cast a Warlock spell that is an Enchantment or [Illusion](app://obsidian.md/Illusions.md), you can do so without Verbal or Somatic components.

### Bonus Actions

***Clairvoyant Combatant (1/day or expend a use of Pact Magic).*** ORO forms a telepathic bond with one creature they can see within 30 ft. They may force the target to make a Wisdom save (DC 26). *Failure:* The target has disadvantage on attack rolls against ORO and ORO has advantage on attack rolls against them. The effect ends after 20 minutes, when the distance to the target exceeds 1+ mile, or when ORO chooses to end it.

***Misty Step.*** ORO casts [[Misty-Step]] (level 2), using a Sorcerer spell slot.

### Reactions

***Shield.*** ORO casts [[01-Rules/Spells/Core/Level 1/Shield|Shield]], using a Sorcerer spell slot.
