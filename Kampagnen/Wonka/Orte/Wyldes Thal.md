Has, due to some unknown curse, formed a ring around the city of Noth, in the form of the [[Wyldwald]]. Travelers to (but not from) Noth must pass through. The dangers are hard to measure. 
Many waystations occupy the roads into the forests, looking to make a profit by selling their assistance to travelers. Teleportation is theoretically available, though much too expensive for most mortals. Mercenaries hang around, hoping to score enough for an early retirement before the job gets the better of them, and there are always some travelers looking to join up in a caravan for extra security.
Flare guns function as distress signals within the woods, though it is made clear before entering that no outside search party will usually enter the woods.
Animals and supplies are sold as well, of course.
