A [sentient fibre from space](https://kill-la-kill.fandom.com/wiki/Life_Fibers), luring and subjugating hosts by weaving itself into seductively powerful magic clothing.

It's prime avatar and most well-known body, representing it in [[Die 7 - SIEBEN]], is an incarnation of [Odin](https://www.enworld.org/threads/epic-monsters-odin.675067/). He is not a fully ignorant victim, but chose to sacrifice his autonomy to fuse with a being that travelled the stars in his insatiable quest for knowledge.

Die Allfaser has many hosts throughout the plane. It's "uniforms", at their most basic, grant increased physical attributes, heightened awareness and temp hp. They can usually drain their host's blood to enhance power, and may puppet their hosts even when they are unconscious or similar.
