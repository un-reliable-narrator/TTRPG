Flache Hierarchien

# Attribute
Immunities: None
Resistances: None

**Impulsive**
Advantage on Initiative, cannot be surprised.

**Weird**
Reaction: When a spell is cast that targets a creature, if you see the caster, you may change the target to another creature you can see.
OR
Reaction: When a spell is cast, if you see the caster, determine a random spell known by the caster. This spell is cast instead. If the new spell targets a creature or are, it targets you or the space you are on. If you are not a valid target, the valid target closes to you is targeted instead. 
For any other considerations required by the spell, you count as the caster.

**Curse**
Cast a random spell on death. Targets your space or the creature who slew you.



Spells (?)
Message, Alter Self/Shapechange, Invisibility, Minor Illusion, Misty Step, Summon Fiend, Unseen Servant, Prestidigitation, Stinking Cloud, Polymorph, Giant Insect, Dimension Door
