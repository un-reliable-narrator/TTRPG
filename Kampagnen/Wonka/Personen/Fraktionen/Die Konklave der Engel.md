*An incarnation of K6BD's [Angels](https://killsixbilliondemons.fandom.com/wiki/Angels). The [Inevitables](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Inevitable) found on [Mechanus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mechanus)* are a poor imitation.

# Statblocks
*In spite of all everything, each Angel is unique.*

Imagine that Angels constantly have Stoneskin, Mindblank, Divine Favor and Shield of Faith affecting them. This is reflected in their statblocks.


Immunities: Necrotic, Poison, Psychic // Charmed, Blinded, Frightened, Petrified, Poisoned, Unconscious, Exhaustion
Resistances: Cold, Fire, Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing (Stone Skin), Damage from Spells (Magic Resistance)
Vulnerable: None
AC: 18+
Legendary Resistances

**Antimagic Field.** As a bonus action, an Angel may activate or end a spell-like effect identical to that of the [[Antimagic-Field]]-spell, centered on it. 

**Magic Resistance**. Angels have advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.

**Immutable Form.** Immune to any effect that would alter its form.

**Forged Enforcers.** Angels are immune to spells or effects that would sense their emotions or thoughts, affect their mind or gain information on them. (Functions like permanent [Mind Blank](https://www.5esrd.com/database/spell/mind-blank).)

Angel Spell List: Paladin Spell List, every spell whose name contains "Word" or "Smite" or "Dominate"

Antimagic Field, Clairvoyance, Command, Conjure Celestial, Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Shield, Divine Smite, Banishing Smite, Summon Celestial, Revivify, Resurrection, Scrying, Zone of Truth, Mass Cure Wounds, Word of Recall, Word of Radiance, Every Power Word, Wall of Force, Time Stop
