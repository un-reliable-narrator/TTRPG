Demonic Entities modelled after a mixture of the Horseman of the Apocalypse, Warhammer's Chaos Gods and Worm's Endbringers

###### Old Guard
- Plague
- Death
- Famine
- War
###### Newcomers
- Pyramid (*Law, Hierarchy, Tyranny*)
- Sparkle (*Money, Gold, Greed, Capitalism*)
- Belonging // Community (*Groupthink, Othering, Fascism*)
- Clockwork (*"Progress", "Rationality"*)
- Doom(er) (*"Nihilism", Pessismism, Cynicism*)
