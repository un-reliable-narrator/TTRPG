### Traits

***Magic Resistance.*** You have advantage on saving throws against spells.

***Serpentine Spellcasting.*** You know the [poison spray](Poison-Spray.md) cantrip. You can cast [animal friendship](Animal-Friendship.md) an unlimited number of times with this trait, but you can target only snakes with it. Starting at 3rd level, you can also cast [Suggestion](Suggestion.md) with this trait. Once you cast it, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. You can also cast it using any spell slots you have of 2nd level or higher.

Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma is your spellcasting ability for these spells when you cast them with this trait (choose when you select this race).

***Divination Savant (Diviner Wizard 3)***
Choose two Wizard spells from the Divination school, each of which must be no higher than level 2, and add them to your spellbook for free.

In addition, whenever you gain access to a new level of spell slots in this class, you can add one Wizard spell from the Divination school to your spellbook for free. The chosen spell must be of a level for which you have spell slots.

***Portent (Diviner Wizard 3)***
Glimpses of the future begin to press on your awareness. Whenever you finish a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md), roll two d20s and record the numbers rolled. You can replace any [D20 Test](app://obsidian.md/d20-test.md) made by you or a creature that you can see with one of these foretelling rolls. You must choose to do so before the roll, and you can replace a roll in this way only once per turn.

Each foretelling roll can be used only once. When you finish a [Long Rest](app://obsidian.md/long-rest.md), you lose any unused foretelling rolls.
