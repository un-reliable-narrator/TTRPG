1/rest: Burn a spell slot to add +5 ft. per level of the burned slot to ANY distance stated in the spell's description (e.g. range, radius, etc.)

Ein Traum, in dem Fizzwick Schmerzen an seiner Wunde hat und sich daraus heraus eine Schlange zu winden scheint. Hautfetzen blättern herab. 

the diamond given by leo is a magic item requiring attunement and revolving around sorcery points and crits
leo tried to hide it with artur (fruitlessly)
the debt collectors are with a powerful guild, the three who come to collect are all buff and imposing and demomic, one tiefling, one or and one dragonkin, only one of them will take the  group down and it will come as a shock
they dont kill as a rule (no profit from the dead, resurrection is expensive)
either they negotiate out, or they are sentenced and stripped of assets and status or they are successfully busted out by leo or even one of their other patrons
only if they escape successfully with leos help and their own efforts will artur manage to keep the diamond (at this point he will have used it extensively
the collectors are based on the lawyers from pacf and the zoldycks

der lachende esel are members of carnivarium and consist of 4 lv3 adventurers: a bard, a rogue, a warlock and a gunslinger

boringcompany is a human fighter guild

kuzan explains the concept of xp to the group: "dont you feel like your training suddenly paid off in a kind of absurd jump?"
he concludes how murder and violence fuels this rotten world. adventurers are god's most dangerous creation, not angels, not humanoids, etc, the strongest ability in the world is not raw power, not broken spells, not the indomitable human spirit and ingenuity, but the potential to level up, and adventurers run on killing

that's what the grand tournament is for: as a bottleneck to limit adventurer populations, by killing many and making them go through hoops for certifications


die goldene hand is another guild

Mending: Can be upcasted to provide 1d6xlevel hp to constructs, otherwise grants adv on checks to stabilize
