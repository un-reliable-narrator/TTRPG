19.12.25 Several "rival" adventurers are at the roadhouse, hoping to make the trip through the greenbelt/feyjungle/cursed canyon/whatever. **Sang- und Klangvoll.** A young team, consisting of *Sang* and *Klang*, both lv1 Lyndwurm bards. They're hoping that the tournament's fame may qualify them for a spot in _Circus Megamus_. They're not expecting to win, or get into the 17. Since one of them is small, they like to go into combat with Sang carrying Klang mounted on their shoulders. They are generally friendly, and very willing to partner up. If one of them dies, the other will likely abandon their quest in grief. They'll give out bardic inspiration, and, if it seems serious, use buff spells _before_ a battle. In combat, they use vicious mockery against targets where the debuff matters, or to finish weakened enemies. **Herbstblatt.** Lv3 Moon Druid travelling on their own. Distant attitude, though possibly interested in Fizzwig. **'Kapitän' Seestern.** Fraudulent caravan master, leading the cheapest trips with the worst reputation. Cheap, in this case, means they're basically free, but you forfeit your possessions in case of death. Has cut a grim deal with the Fey. In other news: _Der 8. Pfad_ (or maybe someone else?) has grasped a powerful secret: The industrial mass production of powerful magic items is an equalizer, "democratizing" levels of power that could kill an adventurer. This way, armies can be raised, and the Next War ignited.
