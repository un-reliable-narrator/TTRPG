# Spells

- [Mending](https://www.thievesguild.cc/spells/spell?spellid=873) gains additional text:
###### **AT HIGHER LEVELS**
By expending a spell slot, you may cast mending as an action to repair an object or a construct creature for 1d4 HP per spell slot level.

# Species

### [Warforged](Warforged.md)  
*Summary:* You are a true robot now. 

- The following trait is changed:
**Construct Resilience.** You are immume to poison damage and the poisoned condition, and magic can't put you to sleep.

- The following trait is added:
**Mechanical Nature.** You cannot eat or drink. You do not gain effects from potions or other items requiring organic ingestion. Further, you are unaffected by most magical healing and can't be targeted by the Medicine skill or a [Healer's Kit](https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/49-healers-kit). 
Instead, a creature may attempt to stabilize you by making a DC 10 Intelligence (Arcana) check. Smith's Tools and Tinker's Tools can be utilized as an action to repair you for 1d10 HP (DC 15).
