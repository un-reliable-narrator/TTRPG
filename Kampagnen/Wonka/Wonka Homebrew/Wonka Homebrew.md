# Feats

###### Fuel the Pact
Req. at least 2 Monk class levels and one Warlock class level.

You may cast spells on your warlock spell list by spending Focus Points in place of spell slots. You may cast the spell at any level up to that of your highest level Warlock spell slot, costing one Focus Point per level.

###### Primal Cunning
Req. 2+ Druid levels and 1+ Rogue level(s)

Once on your turn, when you deal sneak attack damage, you may regain a use of your Wild Shape feature.
