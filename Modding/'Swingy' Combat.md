*Alternate Title: Cinematic Combat*

*Background:* Listening to Crit Role's Campaign 4, I suspect some NPC's had their hp reduced, or a commoner statblock applied where it wasn't necessarily the intuitive first assumption. That is to say: Some people just died. Realistically, from something legitimately lethal (to a real person, but not a DnD creature with 15+hp).  It usually made the situation, better, and more interesting. 
All in all, I had more fun listening to Combats that were resolved quickly, with each swing (and initiative) mattering, potentially taking a combatant before they could act, than those that felt long and involved, dragging on past their peak.
*Conclusion:* Find some easy adjustments to make enemies less durable and more dangerous.

## Solutions

### Minions 
Certain "fodder" creatures don't need hp. It's perfectly fine for them to go down in one hit. It's certainly great if their hp don't have to be tracked. If you don't want them to be taken out by low damage, consider the hp threshold from 4e, or a trait like the Con save to survive that [[Zombie]]s get.

This may be the single most important and impactful adjustment. 

### "Fudging" Hit and Damage Dice
Just use the lowest possible value for hit dice and the highest possible value for damage rolls. For example, a [[01-Rules/Statblocks/Humanoids/Guard|Guard]] would have 4 hp instead of 11, and deal 7 damage instead of an average 4.
The most tempting benefit of this approach is that it doesn't require altering statblocks at all. The most glaring issue is that the effect on monster's hp is quite drastic, and will weaken anything but minions quite severely.

If you already use average damage to speed things up, this is significantly easier to adopt. 

### Increasing AC
...while lowering hp. Increasing saves as well, possibly. 
Thus making it more about *if* you hit, and less about how hard.

This in itself does not make monsters more deadly. Also, I lack a cohesive and comfortable way to blanket-apply this. 

### Something Something Death Saves
What if you gave (some) enemies death saves, but removed going unconscious while dying?
Creatures could keep fighting (and gaining failed death saves upon taking damage), while bleeding out from their wounds. I like the idea that a creature may be conscious and dangerous, but not able to survive their injuries long term. 

If you did this, you'd probably have to remove stabilizing on 3 successful saves. Instead, you only stabilize when given medical attention, and it always returns you to 1 hp (or renders/requires you unconscious).

Maybe you get the choice to lose consciousness, in return for advantage on death saves?
