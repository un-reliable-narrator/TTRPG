# Thoughts on Arcana Unleashed - Deadfall

- I like the descriptions of Thay
- The Eltabbar chapter/missions feel like they should be lower level/CR. Facing Red Wizards should be scary, but you're mostly fighting gangsters, mooks, goons - with more powerful statblocks than an average [[Mage]] Red Wizard. Why is everyone a Warrior Commander, Spy Master or Bandit Crime Lord?
- Szass Tam's statblock suffers from the Lich statblock problems - he should cast more spells. Mass Raise Dead?
- In an adventure centered around magic items, Thrul's and Tam's statblocks list an [[Orb]] and a [[Component-Pouch|Component Pouch]], respectively, under Gear? Magic Items should be a primary feature on enemy statblocks
- More Statblocks for Thayan Knights and Guard Members, please?
- Skeletons? [[Skeleton]]s?? The party will kill six skeletons in a single action and be bored for 2 rounds before the [[Death-Tyrant|Death Tyrant]] shows up. Hell, the civilians could handle those skeletons themselves. Replace with screeching [[Flaming-Skeleton|Flaming Skeleton]]s (whose Death Burst will kill civilians, especially if triggered on turn 1) and scale up the disaster by describing more skeletons in the distance. Optional: If you're scaling down this chapter, or feel weird about a Death Tyrant (created by Szass Tam by accident? A 19 Int monster attacking a random bazaar in a random suicide mission with an "army" of 6 skeletons?), replace it with a [[Beholder-zombie|Beholder Zombie]] (but maybe give it the Negative Energy Cone, so killed civilians rise to fight the party). 
- So "Malefites" are infused with abyssal energy? How does it affect their statblocks? -> Just give them all magic resistance. It's fitting cause Demons have it, and it would make them more scary to Arcane Thay. Optional: Add Cold, Fire, Lightning resistance. Further, make them register as both Undead and Fiend.
- Has Xelnene Thrul ever heard of the term counter-espionage? The whole spy thing is a joke. I'd make Thrul and Wrynn far more paranoid, secretive and ruthless. Witnesses may need to be eliminated. 
- Lots of potential in the *before* the start of the campaign, i.e. *how* the characters get involved. 
- players should have more agency. as it is, they are just Thrul's muscle. they don't even give her very important information
- as someone said: more interesting if Thrul does NOT know about the ritual
- Xelnene Thrul and her presumable myriad resources present more problems than chances. One may scale down Operation Deadfall, make it more of a bottom-up revolution and more tenuous. Thrul becomes a potential tool/ally either as a typical power-conscious Zulkir who hates Szass or a genuine reformer without revolutionary ambition who is responsible for the status quo due to her compliant role in the establishment, like so many disappointing politicians
	- Idea: Have Wrynn be an Agent of the Ninth Quill - in it for the artifacts and money initially
- the Codenames feel forced, silly, edgy



***Large Operation.*** I can understand the party being recruited, as well as Thrul and Wrynn staying in an administrative position. But why would the party be Thrul's main asset, the only ones entrusted with all the major missions? As Tharchion, she commands considerable resources (including an army, even if she does not trust her subordinates). Surely, even if she is deliberately using adventurers as "external" agents with few traces back to her (though that is void with how bluntly she divulges her identity), she can afford to hire multiple groups of adventurers? 
My players don't need to be at the center of everything always.
These would act as rivals to the party, pursuing off-screen missions (scaling up Operation Deadfall and giving it more gravity), as well as missions the PCs decline. As objectives are completed, other operatives may be discarded, eliminated or delegated to less important duties, based on their performance, and the PCs might become increasingly central to the network (being granted increasing insight).

***Security.*** The PCs don't learn Thrul's identity initially. Maybe not even Wrynn's identity, until they prove useful by completing an introductory job. 

## On Espionage
**Thoughts, unfiltered**
- The fallback "clean-up" options are a decent idea and safeguard for the DM. they're also somewhat silly, very gamey and implemented with the same "players are dumb and we must not let them fail"-cowardice that spawns modern AAA game tutorials and quest markers. they should not be repeatable, and not presented as dialogue options proposed by an npc, but have potential if implemented dynamically. 

## Missions

### Militia
- Feels rushed
- Rewriting the campaign to make the party members of the militia feels interesting. Maybe start the adventure with the attack on the keep. 
- Could use some more low-CR combatants? E.g. Guards/Warriors for the Militia and Malefites, Armanite, Babau, Barlgura, Bulezau
- Why does Thrul need the militia? For soldiers, she should have a Tharchion's army. For operatives, she has the party (and her faceless implied spy network). For information/notification of developments, a single, low-value operative with a sending stone should be cheaper and safer.
	- Makes more sense if **A)** Thrul is not involved, e.g. replaced by Wrynn. (this may solve a lot of issues, and make everything more exciting, see above) and **B)** the mission is to support/reinforce/salvage the militia, or gather a more thorough report than would be safe through a sending stone
