# Exhaustion Rules Workshop

## Ideas
*These are independent thoughts. Some may synergize, some will conflict.*

1. Fuse Exhaustion and Death saves.
2. Your Exhaustion scales with your Con modifier (e.g. 3 + con mod)
3. Apply 1 exhaustion when going down / after a combat where you went down
4. Use exhaustion to power a number of effects.
	1. E.g. martial maneuvers.
	2. Alternative: Use death saves to power effects, e.g. reducing/ignoring exhaustion. Think of death saves as long term health and exhaustion as short term? No, that makes little sense.
5. Replace scaling modifier to d20 tests.
	1. Have other abilities/spells, etc. scale (negatively) with user's or target's exhaustion.
	2. Create an *Exhausted* condition (e.g. disadvantage on d20 checks, halved movement speed, etc.) that kicks in when your exhaustion levels exceed a threshold (probably your con mod)
