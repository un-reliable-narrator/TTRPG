# Pokémon

***Levels.*** A Pokémon's species functions somewhat like a character class. Pokémon will only gain levels in their current species, but can change their current species by evolving if they meet the prerequisites. A trainer character advances by acquiring badges, and may in some cases acquire a feat 

***Pokéballs*** function much like a [[Dominate-Monster]] scroll, combined with a [[01-Rules/Spells/Core/Level 8/Demiplane|Demiplane]] to store the Monster. Issuing commands consumes your action, however, and Pokémon statblocks usually have no bonus actions or reactions.

***Moves*** function much like spells, cast by your Pokémon.

#### Feats
**Double Battler.** Your skill in Pokémon battles allows you to split your focus between two Pokémon. In battle, you can maintain concentration on 2 of your Pokémon at the same time. When you use an action to command, you can issue a command to each of them. 
(In regulated battling, single-battle trainers will usually refuse to let their Pokémon fight yours 2 on 1.)
