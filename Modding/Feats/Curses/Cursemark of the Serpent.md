## Cursemark of the Serpent
Your body has, voluntarily or not, become host to a primal spirit of the serpent domain. This will grant you advantages, and you may be tempted to draw on it's power. But the spirit inside you is primal and hungry. It feeds off your energy and threatens to take control of you.

### A TWISTING SCAR
*Cursemark of the Serpent Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+)*

***Snake Eyes.*** While rolling at advantage or disadvantage, if you roll the same number on two dice, you may treat the result as if you had rolled the highest number on the die.

***Shed your skin.*** You have advantage on Death saves. If you regain consciousness as the result of a critical success on a Death save, the Curse advances. (When the curse advances, gain a Cursemark of the Serpent Feat.)

### YOUR BLOOD ITCHES
*Cursemark of the Serpent Feat (Prerequisite: A TWISTING SCAR Feat)*

***Writhing Veins.*** You are resistant to poison damage and have advantage on saves against the [[Poisoned]] condition. 

***Poison Glands.*** During a long rest, you may create a number of poison doses equal to your Constitution modifier (min. 1). As a [Bonus Action](bonus-action.md), apply a poison dose to a weapon or piece of ammunition. Once applied, the poison retains its potency for 1 minute or until you deal damage with the poisoned item, whichever is shorter. When a creature takes damage from the poisoned item, that creature must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Constitution modifier and [Proficiency Bonus](Proficiency.md)) or take `dice: 2d8` Poison damage and have the [Poisoned](Conditions.md#Poisoned) condition until the end of your next turn.
*Optional: If you have a feature that would let you damage a creature using teeth or fangs (e.g. **Vampiric Bite.** from the Dhampir species), you can force a creature to make the above save in addition to dealing damage. Use this feature once per short or long rest.

### YOUR JOINTS BEND IN STRANGE WAYS
*Cursemark of the Serpent Feat (Prerequisite: A TWISTING SCAR Feat)*

***Uncanny Flexibility.*** You have advantage on saves against being grappled. When initiating a grapple, you may use Dexterity instead of Strength to determine the save DC. 

***Slither.*** You can squeeze through any opening that a creature of a size category below yours could fit through.

### FRACTURES ON YOUR SKIN
*Cursemark of the Serpent Feat (Prerequisite: A TWISTING SCAR Feat)*

***Blossoming Scales.*** While not wearing any armor, your base armor class is 14. Once per short or long rest, as a reaction, you may halve an instance of incoming bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage.

### YOUR EYES TURN REPTILIAN
*Cursemark of the Serpent Feat (Prerequisite: A TWISTING SCAR Feat)*

***Ambush Predator.*** 

***Paralyzing Glare.*** As a bonus action, by making eye contact with a living (non-Construct, non-Undead) creature of your size or smaller, you may force them to make a Wisdom save (DC equal to 8 + your Charisma modifier + your PB) or be [[Frightened]] for 1 minute or until they take damage.
A creature may use a reaction to avert their gaze to succeed automatically on this save. While their gaze is averted, they have disadvantage on attacks against you, and your attacks against them are made at advantage.
