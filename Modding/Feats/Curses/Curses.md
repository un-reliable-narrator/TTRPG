*A Curse is a series of feats that are not chosen at level-up. Like a path, a Curse has a core, "initiate"-style feat and contains mechanics/conditions causing it to "advance". When a Curse advances, it's bearer rolls for another random Curse feat whose prerequisites they fulfill. (I.e. a Curse feat table may have the bearer roll 1d8, where any option already rolled is "replaced" by another, more severe feat, introducing drawbacks slowly.)*
