# Path of the Goblin Warlord


## Path of the Goblin Warlord Feats

Goblin Warlords lead a Goblin Band — A travelling group of Goblinoids[^1].

They share these attributes:
- They travel with you/your party, or in your wake. Barring significant obstacles, some or all of them are always nearby.
- They are willing to support you in combat, to an extent. Occasionally, they may even follow your orders, but their loyalty is not absolute.
- They will act out and get you in trouble.

###### Goblin Warrior
*Path of the Goblin Warlord Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, knows language: Goblin)

You gain the following benefits. 
***Ability Score Increase.*** Increase your Dexterity or Charisma score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
***Bree-yark!*** You can call on members of your band to join you in combat. The number and type of creatures ready and willing to fight alongside you at any given time varies, but will generally increase as you accrue renown and progress the path of the Goblin Warlord.
Creatures called by this ability have the following traits:
- Death Save (x1). If reduced to 0 hit points, immediately makes a death save. *Success:* Unconscious and stable. *Failure:* Death. *Massive Damage* (where, after reducing the target to 0hp, the remaining damage equals or exceeds their hit point maximum) rule applies, and will kill without a save.
- When dividing experience points from combat, these creatures don't count toward the number of combatants on your side. (Think of them like a summon or familiar.)

[^1]: Usually.
