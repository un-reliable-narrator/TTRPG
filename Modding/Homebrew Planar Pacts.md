# Clockwork Pact
*(Mechanus Planar Pact)*

Source: Dtzik's Homebrew

*Planar Pact Feat (Prerequisite: Can't Have Another Planar Pact Feat)*

**Immutable Soul.** You have advantage on saves against spells or effects that would alter your form. 

**Clockwork Workings.** You know the [[Mending]] cantrip and learn one other cantrip of your choice from the [[list-spells-school-transmutation|Transmutation]] or [[list-spells-school-enchantment|Enchantment]] school of magic. Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

**Structured Thinking.** When you roll a 5 or lower on the d20 for an Intelligence (Arcana) or (History) check, you can take a Rection to reroll the check, and you must use the new roll. You can''t use this benefit again until you finish a Long Rest.
