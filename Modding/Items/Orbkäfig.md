Ein Ball, der in deine Handfläche passt. Er scheint aus zwei Halbkugeln zu bestehen, doch er lässt sich nicht öffnen oder teilen. Eine Hälfte ist dunkel eingefärbt, die andere zeigt eine Abbildung des Herzes einer unbekannten Spezies.
Dort, wo die Hälften sich treffen, verläuft eine dünne Einbuchtung, an deren Seite sich ein Knopf befindet. Der Knopf hat keine Wirkung im schlafenden Zustand.

As an action, you may toss the Orb at a creature within 30 ft. *(no roll).* The creature makes a Dexterity saving throw (DC 18), choosing to catch the Orb or deflect it to a space within 15 ft. on a success. On a failure, the Orb lands in the creature's space and opens, immediately disintegrating it and storing an exact copy inside the pocket dimension within the Orb.
The copy is exactly identical, including any conditions, damage taken, internal states, memories, intentions, and it's current initiative.
It will take it's turn within the pocket dimension as normal, making a save to escape at the start of their turn. The DC of this save is 18 and the creature may use any saving throw they are proficient with. On a success, they escape. On a failure, their turn ends. Additionally, if they have already failed the save to escape twice, or failed it once and are bloodied, or if they are both bloodied and affected by a harmful condition, the Orb locks permanently. The occupant no longer takes any turns or actions while inside.
You may perform a ritual on an awakened orb, spending 10 minutes and a number of healing potions equal to the occupant's CR to have them fully recover all hp, exhaustion, spell slots and abilities, as well as recover from harmful conditions.

If the Orb is destroyed, the occupant is released.

While the Orb contains a creature, it is Awakened. It may not contain more than one creature, and will never attempt to capture another one. The current occupant may be released by pressing the button. While released, the Orb's occupant must obey the commands of the creature who released them, unless their CR or character level is lower than that of the occupant. The occupant acts on it's master's turn, and the master can use a bonus action to command it to move and/or use one of the actions in it's statblock.

If the released occupant is within 60 ft. of the Orb, pressing the button again will recall them into the Orb.
