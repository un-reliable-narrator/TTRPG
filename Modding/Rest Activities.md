
**What is this addressing?** The new design trend of letting you expend hit dice for damage, etc. is cool. I would personally expand upon it in my homebrew, using HD as a resource for martial maneuvers, etc. The concern, however: Short Rests become irrelevant (again). In 2014, parties wouldn't take short rests because no relevant resources were replenished that way. 2024 introduced more features benefitting from short rests, but now threatens to undermine healing via hit dice (because I may prefer to expend them on damaging enemies).

**The Solution.** Support features to be specifically used *during* a (short) rest. This is easy to make, and can be modelled on/inspired by the campfire abilities of Darkest Dungeon. 

## Rest Activities
*Examples*

Partaking in rest activities does not add to the time required to benefit from a Rest, unless their total time required exceeds that of the Rest.

###### ***Dress Wounds.*** 
**Time Required:** 15 minutes / 30 if targeting self
**Targets:** 1 resting party member
During a Rest, you can spend 15 minutes and 3 charges from a [[Healers-Kit|Healer's Kit]] and make a Medicine check (DC 10) to treat another resting creature's wounds. Alternatively, you can treat your own wounds, doubling the time required. *Success:* You heal the target for 2d6 hp. 

###### ***Hand out Snacks.*** 
**Time Required:** 5 minutes
**Targets:** Up to 5 resting party members
You've prepared snacks, spending 1 sp each. A creature who consumes a snack regains 1 hp and has advantage on saves and checks against exhaustion levels for 4 hours.
