# Homebrew Rules

###### Spell Scrolls
*Spell scrolls can be used by anyone, not just those who know the inscribed spell.*
To cast a spell you don't know from a scroll, make an Intelligence ([[Arcana]]) check. The DC is equal to 10+ the spell's level. *Failure:* You fail to cast the spell. The scroll is not consumed.

> OPTIONAL
> On a natural 1, the spell scroll is consumed. Roll on the [[Wild-Magic-Surge|Wild Magic Surge]] table (or comparable).
> *(To gain proficiency in the Arcana skill, spend ~2 years studying with access to a sufficiently arcane library, or ~1 year of tutoring from a sufficiently advanced wizard. Or sell your brain to a mind sculptor.)*
