# Lock Joints
*Level 2 Enchantment (Wizard)*

**Casting Time:​** Action
**Range:** 60 ft.
**Components:** V, S, M
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a medium or large creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw have the Paralyzed condition. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save.

Does not affect Aberrations or creatures without bones or joints.
