# Unseen Orchestra
*Level 2 Conjuration (Bard, Warlock, Wizard)*

**Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
**Range:** 60 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a record sheet stained with a bard's tears)
**Duration:** 1 hour

This spell creates an Invisible, mindless, shapeless Huge force that is instantly very, very loud. The concert it produces can be heard within 300 ft. It has AC 10, 15d10 Hit Points (83 average, 1 per ensemble member), a Strength of 2, a +11 to Charisma (Performance) checks and it can't attack. If it drops to 0 Hit Points, the spell ends.
Creatures can move through the Orchestra's space, treating it as [[Difficult Terrain]]. 

***Concert.*** While making it plays, all creatures within a 60 ft. Emanation of the Orchestra are [[Deafened]] and cannot communicate via Telepathy or the [[Message]] cantrip. Within the Emanation, spells requiring a verbal component cannot be cast, as even magical forces cannot understand their instructions over the noise. 
(The DM may have attempts at verbal spellcasting trigger a [[Wild-Magic-Surge|Wild Magic Surge]].)

As an action, any creature within 30 ft. of the Orchestra can make a Strength save (DC equals your spells save DC) to attempt to shout over the noise and ask the Orchestra to play any song or composition. The Orchestra will play it perfectly, granting the petitioner Bardic Inspiration (1d10). Once the piece is finished, the spell ends and the Orchestra disappears.

As a Bonus Action, any creature with a [[Wand-of-Conducting|Wand of Conducting]] can mentally command the Orchestra to play, stop playing, or move up to 10 ft.
