# Fighter 2
*Small or Medium, humanoid (Adventurer)*

**AC** 21 (Plate Armor + Shield + Defense Fighting Style)
**HP** 17 (2d10 + 6)
**Speed** 30 ft.

**Str** 16 (+3) **Dex** 12 (+1) **Con** 16 (+3) **Int** 8 (-1) **Wis** 12 (+1) **Cha** 10 (+0)

**Saves** *Str* +5, *Con* +5
**Skills** *Athletics* +5, *Perception* +2
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP) **PB** +2

### Actions

***[[Longsword]].*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 + 5 Slashing damage and the target has disadvantage on it's next attack roll before the fighter's next turn.

***Warhammer.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 + 5 Bludgeoning damage and the fighter may push the target up to 10 ft. straight away if it is large or smaller.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 1d6 + 5 Piercing damage and the target's speed is reduced by 10 ft. until the fighter's next turn.

***Action Surge (1/Day).*** The fighter takes an additional action, except the Magic action.

### Bonus Actions

***Second Wind (2/Day).*** The fighter regains 1d10 + 2 hp.
